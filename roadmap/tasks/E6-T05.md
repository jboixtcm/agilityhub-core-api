# E6-T05 · `HistoryItem.activityId` (E6-W02's question 1)

## Objective
Screen 25 lists the member's activities together with her classes and trainings (R-10-14). The web cannot link an activity row to the activity's page (`/activitats/{id}`, S07 §2): the row's `id` is the registration's, and `HistoryItem` carries no activity id. This task adds it (ruling E74). Web E6-W04 step 0b links the rows.

**The change comes with a test that fails before it.**

## Spec references
S10 R-10-14 and §6 (`GET /me/history`, amended 30-09: `activityId` on the `ACTIVITY` rows) · S07 §2 and §6 (the member's activity page, `GET /me/activities/{activityId}`: 200 for a `PUBLISHED` or `FINISHED` activity, 404 otherwise) · T-10-19.

## Context to load
- `clubs/bookings/application/HistoryQuery.java` (the `ACTIVITY` branch), `clubs/bookings/application/ports/ActivityHistoryQuery.java` (`Item`), `clubs/activities/application/ActivityHistory.java` and `ActivityQueryService.historyRowsFor` (its rows already hold `activityId`).
- `clubs/bookings/api/InstructorContracts.java` (`HistoryItem`), the E6 contract tests and `docs/openapi/`.

## Steps
1. **`HistoryItem.activityId`** (string, nullable): the activity's id on every `ACTIVITY` row, and `null` on the `CLASS` and `TRAINING` rows. The row's `id` stays the registration's.
   - Carry it from `ActivityQueryService.historyRowsFor` through `ActivityHistoryQuery.Item` to the row.
   - Declare it in the contract (`InstructorContracts.HistoryItem`: nullable, not required), regenerate the snapshot, and add a line to `docs/openapi/CHANGELOG.md` and to `CHANGELOG.md`.
   - Test (T-10-19): a member with a done activity and a registration she cancelled in time → both rows carry the activity's id, and `GET /me/activities/{activityId}` answers 200 to her for each; the class and training rows carry `null`.
2. **Nothing else changes.** The order, the states, the 500 cap and the modules stay as they are (`ACTIVITIES` off → no `ACTIVITY` row).

### Assumptions

1. **Always sent.** `activityId` is sent on every row, with `null` on CLASS and TRAINING rows, like `dogId` on an ACTIVITY
   row. It is not omitted, because the api's other nullable fields of 25 are sent the same way. The schema marks it
   `NOT_REQUIRED` + `nullable`, as the task says.
2. **Every ACTIVITY row carries it.** The step says «on every `ACTIVITY` row», so the CANCELLED_BY_CLUB rows carry it too.
   Whether to link it is the web's call (E74: an activity the club cancelled has no page).
3. **Placement.** The field sits right after `id`, as in S10 §6's example (`"id":"ar1","activityId":"a1"`). The JSON order
   is informative only.
