# E8-T12 · E8-T11's round-2 review follow-ups

## Objective
Fix the two money defects that the review of E8-T11's round 2 found, before the E8 gate (ruling E102). Keep the invariants of E8-T09 and E8-T11 and their generated-sequence test green, and extend the generator so that it reaches both paths.

## Steps
For each point, first write the test that fails without the fix.
1. **Pending dashboard refunds are reserved** (#1, `PaymentRefunds.java:281`). A refund that Stripe reports as `pending` counts in the reservations (`ledger().reserved()`), without counting twice a refund linked to one of our own operations. It also reduces an unbilled credit before billing can consume it. The review's example must end without overpayment: 12 € captured, a CREDIT of 12 €, a 5 € dashboard refund reported pending, billing runs, then the refund succeeds. Test both outcomes of the pending refund (success and failure), and make the generator's own ledger count pending external refunds too.
2. **A failed late refund reaches the admin** (#2, `PaymentRefunds.java:193`). When the `REFUND_LATE` refund of a genuinely expired checkout fails (Stripe's failure, or an exhausted refusal), resolve the payment through the operation's target, not through `upfront.forIntent(intent)`. Then record the intervention with `PAYMENT_REFUND_INTERVENTION`, `UpfrontRefundIntervention` and N-55, with the late capture as the amount owed. Test an actual expired checkout whose late refund fails. The generator's «late confirmation» starts from a `PAID` payment and never reaches this path, so add the expired case.
