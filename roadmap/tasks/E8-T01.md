### Assumptions
1. **`PackBalance` naming.**
   - The S12 pack lives in `payments`, as the task's fixed conventions say. The glossary's `clubs/billing` does not exist.
   - Its wire schema is **`PackBalanceDetail`**, because `PackBalance` is already S08's booking summary in the snapshot (the E5-T01 `RiskReviewForm` precedent). The collection and document are `pack_balances` / `PackBalance`.
2. **The creditor IBAN is never in a response.**
   - `Remittance.creditor` exposes `maskedIban`, and `Remittance` exposes `fileAvailable` instead of `fileKey` (`GET /remittances/{id}/file` gives the signed URL).
   - In storage, `Remittance.creditor.iban` is `@Sensitive` (MASK_IBAN).
3. **`LeaveSource`** is `MEMBER · ADMIN · PACK_EXPIRED` (task and S13 §3) plus the model's `MIGRATED`. Annex B writes `REQUEST` for `MEMBER`, and I followed the spec and the task. Same for `MemberLeftReason`, which also has `MIGRATED`, because E2-T10 writes it.
4. **Business dates and months** are ISO strings in storage (`YYYY-MM`, `YYYY-MM-DD`, club-local) and `LocalDate`/pattern-checked strings on the wire. Instants are UTC. Money is always `Money`.
5. **Dependency direction.** `payments` reaches the census only through the existing `shared.application` ports (`MemberIdentityAccess`, `DogOwnerAccess`, `TeamMemberAccess`, `SignupCapabilities`), never through `clubs.census.application`.
   - Reason: `clubs.census.application.SignupService` and `DemoSignupSeeder` already call `payments.application` (E3-T03), so the reverse edge would be a context cycle.
   - The new rules encode this: `PAYMENTS_CLUB_DEPENDENCIES` and `CENSUS_THROUGH_PORTS`, with those two E3 classes as the only allowed census→payments callers.
6. **Deferred list fields.** `GET /members`' new S13 filters are published but answer `501` through a new `ListDefinition.Field.deferred(type)`.
   - Filtering on a path nothing computes yet would silently return wrong rows.
   - E8-T05 replaces `deferred` with the real path.
7. **`Club.billing` is a record component**, not a loose field: a full club save and `club:apply` keep the invoice counter. A test proves it and fails without the fix (§2a).
8. **The Stripe settings are read typed** from `paymentProviders.STRIPE` (`StripeProviderSettings`).
   - The `*Enc` secrets are `@JsonIgnore @Sensitive(HIDE)`, are left out of `toString`, and are left empty in seeds.
   - Nothing in the repo holds a key.
9. **`BankAccountVault`** is declared and tested but has no caller yet. The SEPA writer (E8-T03) is its only intended reader; every other reader keeps `maskedAccount`/`ibanLast4`.
10. **`POST /members/{id}/plan-change`** (S12 §6, global-review proposal of 26-09) is **not** published. S12 itself assigns it to E8-T05 step 10, and this task's step 1 does not list it.
