# E11-T01 · RGPD

## Objective
Implement S14's WP-D (ROADMAP E11-T01, size XL; E11 is never cut): the member's data package, the right to erasure and its executor, the monthly retention sweep, security events, consents and the platform views.

## Spec references
`docs/specs/S14-tauler-auditoria-exportacions-rgpd.md`: R-14-13…17, the erasure state table, the erasure, data-export, consents and `/platform/*` rows of the endpoints table, WP-14-D, and T-14-08, 09, 17…21 and 24. `docs/INCIDENCIES_OBERTES.md`: INC-10 (the purge) and point 10 of INC-53.

## Context to load
`AGENTS.md` · `docs/MODEL_DADES_PLATAFORMA.md` (what each collection holds) · E7-T04's `announcements` collection (its `q` and `memberIds` are erased too) · S12's `forgetCustomer` · S13's `LEFT` state.

## Steps
For each rule, first write the test that fails without it, named after the spec id.
1. **Data package (R-14-13, N-50).** `POST /members/{id}/data-export` and `POST /me/data-export` create an asynchronous `ExportJob{kind: MEMBER_DATA}`; the ZIP holds `data.json` (`memberData.v1`) and the member's files; N-50 when it is ready (T-14-17).
2. **Erasure request and eligibility (R-14-14).** Both endpoints, `ErasureEligibility` and its reasons, `422 ERASURE_BLOCKED`, the `409`s and the cancellation (T-14-09, T-14-18, T-14-24).
3. **Execution (R-14-15).** `ErasureExecutor` pseudonymises field by field per the spec's table, one Mongo transaction per club, `AccountErased` to the outbox, the S3 cleanup by a retrying consumer, idempotent on `erasedAt` (T-14-08, T-14-19). It also covers `announcements` and every collection added since S14 was written: list them, collection by collection, in the report against the model.
4. **Retention (R-14-16).** The monthly `RetentionSweep` (an S15 job with its `SchedulerRun` trace), INC-10's purge included (T-14-20).
5. **Security events (R-14-17).** The `SecurityEvent` writers in S01, the rate limiter and S12; never the e-mail, only `emailHash` (T-14-21).
6. **Consents and platform views.** `/members/{id}/consents` and the `/platform/*` views of WP-14-D.
