### Assumptions
- **Request shape:** one live request per member through a unique partial index on `{memberIds}` with `live: true` (Mongo's partial filters do not take `$in`); `erasedClubIds[]`, `accountErased`, `lastAttemptAt` and `version` (T-14-24's optimistic lock) are technical fields, entered in S14 §3 and Annex B. An `ACCOUNT` request (console) progresses club by club in each club's daily run; the club that completes it also erases the account; an account without members is erased at once, outside any club.
- **`executeAt`:** `max(now, leaveDate + rgpd.erasureMinDaysAfterLeave)` at the club's midnight; an `ACCOUNT` request takes the latest of its clubs; `RETENTION` executes now.
- **Account kept:** when another club keeps an `ACTIVE` membership (R-14-15's last row), or, with `scope = MEMBER`, when the account is federated with Learn (`externalIds.learnUserId`, S14 §13-7); then only the membership is `ERASED`. A member-scoped request does not revoke the account's other clubs' sessions (`AccountErasureHandler`).
- **Pseudonym key:** `hmac16` uses a key derived from the existing server secret (`SignupCapabilities.fingerprint("erasure-pseudonym-key")`); no new secret.
- **Processes:** `erasures` (daily, `jobs.dailyTime`) and `retention-sweep` (monthly, day 1) have no switch: `PUT /jobs/{name}/switch` → `404 JOB_UNKNOWN {reason: NO_SWITCH}`. Both run by hand and in dry run.
- **Retention (R-14-16):** (a) and (b, INC-10) as the rule; (c) a remittance whose XML is older than `rgpd.retentionYearsAfterLeave` keeps everything but `fileKey` and gets `filePurgedAt`; (d) audit older than `audit.retentionYears`; (e) expired exports → `EXPIRED`; (f) security events by their TTL index (`security.eventRetentionDays`, 90).
- **Security events:** `emailHash = fingerprint("email|" + normalised)`, the same HMAC as `Account.emailHash`; the e-mail is never stored; anonymous routes record no address.
- **INC-53 point 10 (and E7-W07 Q3):** an erased member's `birthDate` and `address` are `null` (both `nullable` in `Member`, so optional by the project's convention; the instructor view leaves the address out). `POST /members/{id}/erasure` answers `409 ERASURE_ALREADY_REQUESTED` or `409 MEMBER_ERASED`; `POST /members/{id}/data-export` answers `409 MEMBER_ERASED` for an erased member and `DATA_EXPORT_TOO_SOON` (catalog `422`) inside 24 hours. `impersonation-token` for an erased (and `LEFT`) member answers `409 MEMBER_ERASED` (the erasure check comes first). An erased SEPA member has `accountMissing: false`. The fiche lists the dogs, as «Gos suprimit». The preferences are removed, and `GET /members/{id}/notification-preferences` answers `409 MEMBER_ERASED`, as its contract already declares (neither `{}` nor the defaults).
- **Catalogs (E109):** no new error code, event, notification or parameter (all were in the catalogs already). New: the two R-15-01 rows (S15) and the model fields above.
