## Steps
1. **Lapsed signup checkouts are released by P5's step h (question R2-1; S15 R-15-15, S04 R-04-26).**
   - Move the signup sweep out of P7 into a new step h of P5 (daily), gated by `BILLING`. P7 goes back to bookings only, with `SINGLE_CLASS`.
   - Keep E5-T30's behaviour. A `PENDING` signup session past `expiresAt` → `EXPIRED`, and its rows go back to `DUE`, with or without a provider session and with the provider off.
   - The effect stays `EXPIRE_CHECKOUT`, the dry run `WOULD_EXPIRE_CHECKOUT {checkoutSessionId, minutesExpired}`, and the counter `expiredCheckouts`.
   - Reach the checkout through a port, as booking checkouts go through `SingleClassChargePort` (review #2's design nit).
   - Tests (T-15-23's step h):
     - a club without `SINGLE_CLASS` and with `BILLING` → the session is released;
     - `BILLING` off → the step is skipped;
     - two runs → one effect;
     - a lapsed session of another club never enters the plan (review #7).
2. **PAY_TO_BOOK's late confirmation is tested (review #3).** Add a `SingleClassCheckoutIT` case with two payments that are confirmed after `bookings.paymentPendingMinutes` runs out. Pass a real `paidAt`, not `null`.
   - Paid before the deadline → the booking is confirmed, with no refund mark.
   - Paid after it → E34's mark.
3. **The provider calls' timeout is part of the contract (review #4; CONVENCIONS_API §7, ruling E80).**
   - Write it in `PaymentProvider`'s contract Javadoc, next to the idempotency by `sessionId`: every call's timeout stays well below `IdempotencyRepository.CLAIM_LEASE` (10 min).
   - Give the fake provider's client that timeout, and test that the configured timeout is below the lease.
4. **The review's nits.**
   - #5: in `IdempotencyFilter`, a failure of `records.held(record)` never hides the original failure: treat the record as held and add the error as suppressed.
   - #6: when `abandon`'s key lock fails, record the provider's failure: a WARN with `checkoutSessionId` and `clubId` only (no e-mail), or a suppressed exception.
   - #8: if CONVENCIONS_API §7 gets a test id, name `E5_T30_aStoppedRequests…` after it; otherwise leave the name and say so.
