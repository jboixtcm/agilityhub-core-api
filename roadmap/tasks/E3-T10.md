### Assumptions
1. **Step 1.** With `signup.allowFamilyGroupPending = false`, a FOUND claim that no longer matches still answers `422 FAMILY_HOLDER_NOT_FOUND` («when … allows it»). A claim with `leavePending` and the flag off stays `400`.
2. **Step 3.** A SEPA→SEPA PATCH is partial: the IBAN, holder and holder tax id that the request does not send are kept, and so is `mandateSignedAt`. An explicit `null` clears a field. MANUAL→SEPA is a new mandate.
3. **Step 4.** The consumer deactivates the dogs of `payload.dogIds` that are still PENDING and were submitted no later than the rejection (`signup.submittedAt ≤ occurredAt`). 4. **Step 5.**
