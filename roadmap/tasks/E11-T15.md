# E11-T15 · Pre-launch money fixes (the audit of 21-10)

## Objective
The organizer's pre-launch audit of 21-10 (ruling E145; auditor 1, the money and migration chain of `main` `8d73ad2a`, rules verified with jshell on the compiled domain classes) found two critical and four major defects in the money path, plus five smaller ones. This task fixes them in the audit's order of severity. The three with a product decision carry it: E146 (F1/F3: an audited ADMIN route that sets `Member.nextInvoiceDate`, and a per-member «paid until» override for the migration) and E147 (F5: the `COLLECTION_DATE_PASSED` rollback blocker and the warning the day before the collection date; marking a remittance `SUBMITTED` on download is **not** done — the admin keeps the explicit «Marca com a enviada»). F10 is not code: its three facts go to the runbook.

**Each fix comes with a test that fails before it**: a red log on the old behaviour, then the green log. For a runbook or spec step the evidence is the diff. The audit's file:line references were checked on `8d73ad2a` by the organizer; where the audit was off, the step gives the right line.
