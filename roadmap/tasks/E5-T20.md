## Steps
1. **The ring-conflicts preview (E4-W11 review #1, S07 §6).** `GET /activities/{id}/ring-conflicts` already answers `422 OUTSIDE_OPENING_HOURS` and `400 INVALID_TIME_RANGE` for a window the publication would refuse. Declare both in its `@ContractErrors`. Contract test.
2. **The D11 blocks follow the catalog (E4-W06 question 3).**
   - `learn.baseUrl` and `learn.recommendationsTtlMinutes` are system parameters: not in a club block, and not editable by the club.
   - `signup.onboardingFields` and `legal.maxPostpones` are in the club's «Alta i consentiments» block, editable.
   - The keys of «Històric i tauler» stay system.
   - IT on the blocks of `GET /parameters` and on a club `PUT` of each moved key.
3. **Sparse `fields` (E4-W05 question 1, CONVENCIONS_API §4).**
   - On `GET /activities` and `GET /activities/{id}/registrations`, a key that `fields` did not request is left out, as `GET /members` does. It is never `null` or `false` in place of a value.
   - The row id always comes (`id`, `registrationId`).
   - The list item schemas mark the other properties optional, as `/members`' does.
   - Each universal list publishes the `fields` keys it accepts (`x-fields`), next to `x-filterable` and `x-sortable`.
   - IT and contract test.
4. **A waitlist rank (E4-W05 question 2, S07 R-07-08).** A `WAITLISTED` registration carries `waitlistRank`: its 1-based rank among the active waiting entries of the activity, in `position` order, computed when it is read. `position` keeps the stored order. It is on the D7 registrations list and on the member's views of the activity and of their registrations. IT: after a promotion, the next entry reads rank 1.
5. **`appliedFilters` of the registrations list (E4-W05 question 3).** It leaves out the path's `activityId`, and lists only the filters of the query. IT.
6. **A bad day-grid view (E4-W05 question 5, CONVENCIONS_API §5).** `GET /day-grid?view=foo` answers `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` with `details.field: view` and `fieldErrors`. IT.
