# E3-T17 · E38 for the dog of a readmission

## Objective
The gate E3 re-run (E3-T11) and its review (`roadmap/reviews/E3-T11-20260925-2344-claude.md`, finding #1) found that E38 protects the member record of a readmission, but not the reused dog. The organizer confirmed it in the code, and it goes further than the review says:
- `SignupService.prepareDog` (near `:402`) writes the submitted name, sex, breed, birth date and instructor note onto the LEFT member's INACTIVE dog at submission. It also clears `deactivatedAt` and `deactivationReason`.
- `saveDocuments` (near `:416`) replaces the files of the dog's existing documents, type by type. When no vaccination card is sent, it sets that document to `PENDING` and emits `DogDocumentPending`. The replaced files are then unreferenced, so P9 deletes them after 48 h (S15 R-15-19).
- `reject` (near `:797`) restores only the member (`restoreLeft`). The dog keeps the submitted values, gets `INACTIVE{SIGNUP_REJECTED}` with a new date, and its `signup` block is replaced.

So anyone who knows a former member's DNI and the dog's chip can rewrite the dog record and lose its documents, and a rejection does not undo it. S04 and S15 were amended on 25-09: E38 covers the reused dog, with its documents.

This task also completes two partial proofs of the audit: item 3 (the holder's group) and item 1 (the add-dog replay). **It must be verified before gate E3 closes.** The web shows the dog's old and new values on D2 in a later task, from this task's snapshot.

**Each fix comes with a test that fails before the fix.**

### Assumptions
1. **The reused dog's chip is locked while the readmission is pending.** Changing it gives `409 INVALID_STATE` with `details.reason = READMISSION_PENDING`; sending the chip it has is no edit.
   - This mirrors R-04-06 (b) for the identity document: the readmission matched the dog on its chip.
   - Without the lock, a D2 chip change would write the record, and a rejection would not leave the dog «com era». No new error code or reason is used.
2. **Only the step-17 fields are submitted values in a D2 PATCH.** Name, sex, breed, birth month or date, notes and documents edit the reused dog's request. `handlerName` and `licenses` (S03 fields, not step 17) still edit the record, as the member's `remarks`/`internalNotes` do during a readmission.
3. **While pending, the dog record keeps `deactivatedAt` and `deactivationReason`**, like the member's `leftAt`/`leftReason`. They are also in `previous`. Validation clears them. `GET /dogs/{id}` of a pending reused dog therefore shows them.
4. **D2's `documents` for the reused dog are those the validation will write.** Each type the submission sent replaces the record's document of that type (RECEIVED with files, PENDING without). The record's other types stay, as today's submission does (`writeDocuments` touches only the sent types plus `VACCINATION_CARD`).
   - `DOCUMENT_PENDING` is judged on the same list.
   - `changedFields` has `documents` when a type's state or file keys differ.
5. **N-01 names the submitted dog.** `SignupSubmitted.dogNames` carries the submitted name, as a submission did before this task.
