# E3-T17 · E38 for the dog of a readmission

## Objective
The gate E3 re-run (E3-T11) and its review (`roadmap/reviews/E3-T11-20260925-2344-claude.md`, finding #1) found that E38 protects the member record of a readmission, but not the reused dog. The organizer confirmed it in the code, and it goes further than the review says:
- `SignupService.prepareDog` (near `:402`) writes the submitted name, sex, breed, birth date and instructor note onto the LEFT member's INACTIVE dog at submission. It also clears `deactivatedAt` and `deactivationReason`.
- `saveDocuments` (near `:416`) replaces the files of the dog's existing documents, type by type. When no vaccination card is sent, it sets that document to `PENDING` and emits `DogDocumentPending`. The replaced files are then unreferenced, so P9 deletes them after 48 h (S15 R-15-19).
- `reject` (near `:797`) restores only the member (`restoreLeft`). The dog keeps the submitted values, gets `INACTIVE{SIGNUP_REJECTED}` with a new date, and its `signup` block is replaced.

So anyone who knows a former member's DNI and the dog's chip can rewrite the dog record and lose its documents, and a rejection does not undo it. S04 and S15 were amended on 25-09: E38 covers the reused dog, with its documents.

This task also completes two partial proofs of the audit: item 3 (the holder's group) and item 1 (the add-dog replay). **It must be verified before gate E3 closes.** The web shows the dog's old and new values on D2 in a later task, from this task's snapshot.

**Each fix comes with a test that fails before the fix.**

## Steps
1. **The reused dog waits in the request (R-04-06, E38).** This applies when a readmission sends the chip of the member's own INACTIVE dog.
   - The dog becomes `PENDING` (T-04-12). Its previous `status`, `deactivatedAt`, `deactivationReason` and `signup` block are kept, like the member's.
   - The submitted name, sex, breed, birth month and notes to instructors wait in the request. The dog record keeps its own values until validation.
   - The submitted documents wait too. The dog's existing documents keep their files and states, and the submission emits no `DogDocumentPending` for the reused dog.
   - While the request is pending, its files count as referenced for P9 (R-15-19), so they are not deleted.
   - D2's view shows the submitted values and documents for the dog, and adds the previous value of each changed field. This is additive, like the member's.
   - Validation applies the values and documents as a submission does today, with the same states, events and N-01 values.
   - Choose where the pending values live (for example next to the member's `readmissionRequest`, or on the dog). Say it in the report as a model proposal.
2. **A rejection leaves the reused dog exactly as it was (R-04-23, E38).**
   - The reused dog returns to its previous `status`, `deactivatedAt`, `deactivationReason` and `signup` block, with its own values and documents, files included.
   - The rejection is recorded as the member's is for a readmission: the audit entry and `SignupRejected`. It is not stamped on the reused dog's restored block.
   - A dog that is new in the readmission follows R-04-23 as today: `INACTIVE{SIGNUP_REJECTED}`, with the decision stamped.
   - The submitted files become orphans, and P9 deletes them after 48 h (R-04-06 c: a rejection erases the submitted data).
   - **Tests:**
     - T-04-19: a readmission renames the dog, changes its breed and birth month, sends a new vaccination card and no insurance, and is rejected. The dog document and its `dog_documents` equal the ones from before the submission (`version` and `updatedAt` aside), and P9 still sees the old files as referenced.
     - The same readmission, validated: the dog carries the new values and the new card.
     - T-04-12: while the request is pending, the dog record keeps its old values, and D2's view shows the new ones with the previous values.
     - P9: a pending readmission's file older than 48 h is not deleted; after the rejection, it is.
   - Extend `SignupSecurityFixesIT.T_04_12_T_04_19_rejectedReadmissionLeavesTheRecordExactlyAsItWas` to read the dog and its documents too. Adapt `SignupFollowUpFixesIT` (near `:245`) if it relied on the old behaviour.
3. **Audit item 3: the holder's group (E3-T11 review #2, T-04-16).** In `SignupGateFixesIT.T_04_16_foundFamilyClaimProposesAndValidatesTheHiddenFamilyFare`, assert that the validated member's `familyGroupId` equals the holder's, and that the group lists both members. If it fails, fix the validation.
