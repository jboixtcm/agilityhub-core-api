# E4-T06 · Activity read models for D7 and the app

## Objective
The web task E4-W04 (D7 and the activities in the app) found three gaps in the S07 read models (its report, questions 1, 2 and 4; organizer verification of 24-09):
- the D7 list cannot draw its columns;
- `cancelReason` cannot be `null`;
- an activity without an end time reaches the app with a made-up end.

This small, additive task closes them. The web keeps a `pending.json` overlay until this snapshot is published, and its adoption then prunes it.

## Spec references
- S07 §2, row D7 (the list columns: date and hours, type, rings or «fora del club», registrations «22/40»), R-07-11, R-07-13, and the «Canvis» note of 24-09.
- `CONVENCIONS_API.md` (optional vs nullable; INC-08).

## Steps
1. **`ActivityListItem` gets the D7 columns.** Add `startTime` and `endTime` (local `HH:mm`; `endTime` `null` when the activity has no end), `allRings` (bool), `location` (the free text of an activity away from the club, or `null`), `maxPlaces` (`null` when unlimited) and `typeDisplay` (the resolved label of `type`, or of the free label for `OTHER`). They must be the same values as the activity's own view. Contract test with the four D7 seed activities.
2. **`ActivityRegistrationListItem.cancelReason`** is nullable in its enum (`null` until the registration is cancelled), or it is omitted while null. Pick one, apply it to the OpenAPI and the serializer together, and say which in the report.
3. **No made-up end.** The model keeps its convention (an activity without an end ends the next day at `00:00`, for ordering and for the schedulers). The read models send `endTime: null` / `endsAtLocal: null` for such an activity: `ActivityListItem`, `RegisteredActivity`, and the rows of 03 and 25. Test: a start-only activity («Lliga», 9:00) → `null` end in every read model; an activity with an end is unchanged.

What changed:
- **Step 1.** `ActivityListItem` gains `typeDisplay`, `startTime`, `endTime`, `allRings`, `location` and `maxPlaces`. `location` is the free text of an activity away from the club, otherwise `null`. The list's aggregation projects the raw inputs under the column names. A row hook of the list dataset then finishes them with the view's own helpers in `ActivityProjection`, the ones `GET /activities/{id}` uses: `type(type, label)`, `allRings(ids, activeRingIds)`. So a row and the view cannot disagree. The ring catalog is read once per page, not once per row. `fields=` can select the new columns.
- **Step 2.** One choice for both the OpenAPI and the serializer: **always sent, `null` until it applies.** `cancelReason` is `required`, its type is `string | null`, and its `enum` keeps `null`. The record is `@JsonInclude(ALWAYS)`, so a stricter global default could not drop it. I applied the same rule to its two siblings, `cancelledAt` and `position` (Assumption 1).
- **Step 3.** A new `ActivityProjection.endsAtLocal(a)` returns `null` when `endTime` is `null`. `RegisteredActivity`, the `/me/activities` rows (`ActivityRow`) and the 03 rows (`liveRegistrationsFor` → `/me/home`) all use it. `ActivityListItem.endTime` is the stored `null`. The model is unchanged: `endsAt` is still the next day at 00:00 local, and ordering, liveness on 03, deadlines and `finishEnded` still use it (the test proves it). The 25 rows (`historyRowsFor`) never carried an end, and the `HistoryItem` contract has none (Assumption 3).
