## Steps
1. **S1 (major) — an erased booker's first name stays in other members' bookings.** `clubs/bookings/persistence/BookingErasureRepository.erase:28-31`: the two `$unset …displayName` updates filter `memberId in memberIds` **and** the actor predicate (`bookedBy.accountId in accountIds` or `bookedBy.impersonatedMemberId in memberIds`); the same shape in `clubs/activities/persistence/ActivityErasureRepository.erase:26-28` (`registeredBy`). Scenario: a family-group member books for another member's dog → `bookings {memberId: other, bookedBy: {accountId: booker-acc, displayName: "Booker"}}` (`BookingConfirmationService.java:92` writes `subject.booker().firstName()`); the booker leaves and is erased: `members` and `accounts` pseudonymised, but the other member's booking keeps «Reservada per Booker» for ever; the same for `cancelledBy.displayName` when an instructor-member cancels for someone else (`BookingActors.member:16-20`) and for `activity_registrations.registeredBy.displayName`. The same method already does it right for `attendances.markedBy` (`:34-35`: `accountId` only). Secondary: another applicant's `members.familyGroupClaim.holderName/dogName` naming the erased holder stays (`CensusErasureRepository.eraseMember:37` clears only the erased member's own claim). Rule: S14 R-14-15. Fix: drop `and("memberId").in(memberIds)` from the three actor updates (keep `club()` and the `orOperator`; the `cancelMessage` unset at `:27` and `seat_holds` at `:32` stay member-scoped); in `CensusErasureRepository` clear `familyGroupClaim.holderName` and `.dogName` of every other member of the club whose `familyGroupClaim.holderMemberId` is an erased member (a `FOUND` claim; a `NOT_FOUND_PENDING` claim carries no id and cannot be matched by name: say so in the report). Test (red first): `ErasureIT`'s fixture + a booking `{memberId: "active", bookedBy: {accountId: "erased-account", displayName: "Fictional Name"}}`, a booking of «active» `cancelledBy` the erased account, an activity registration of «active» `registeredBy` it, and a pending applicant whose `familyGroupClaim` names the erased holder → after `ErasureExecutor.execute` the three `displayName` are absent and the claim keeps `status`/`holderMemberId` without the names; today the three names stay (`ErasureIT.java:281` covers only `memberId = erased`). The counts in `ErasureEffect` (`bookings`, `activity_registrations`) include the changed documents.
2. **S2 (minor) — the outbox's `FAILED` records and the job runs.** The audit's main claim («erasure never touches the outbox») is already false on `main`: `PrivacyTraces.erase` deletes the `PUBLISHED` records about the members and dogs (`PrivacyTraceRepository.deleteProcessedEvents:20-25`, E11-T01/E11-T11), and `ErasureIT.java:222,333` asserts it with a `MemberUpdated{diff.firstName}` — say so in the report. What remains: (a) a `FAILED` record (`OutboxDispatcher` after `maxAttempts`) keeps its payload — `MemberUpdated{diff}` with before/after `firstName/lastName/phones/contactEmails/address` (`MemberService.java:129`; only IBAN and id document are masked) — and neither the erasure (`status = PUBLISHED` only) nor the cleanup (`CleanupRepository.java:79-85`: «PENDING/FAILED ones are never touched») ever removes it; (b) a record still `PENDING` at erasure time is published afterwards and lives `jobs.retention.domainEventsDays` (90 d) with its diff; (c) `job_runs.items.detail.attendances[].dogName` of P3's dry run (`NoShowNoticesJob.java:74`) is kept 90 d and, for the last five runs of each process, for ever (`CleanupJob`). Rules: R-14-15 vs R-15-19. Fix: `deleteProcessedEvents` takes `status ∈ {PUBLISHED, FAILED}` (a failed record about an erased subject has no consumer left to serve); the monthly `RetentionSweepJob` (R-15-19) gets a `RetentionTask` in `PrivacyTraces` that deletes the `PUBLISHED`/`FAILED` records about members with `erasedAt != null` (the (b) leftovers) and `$unset`s `items.$[].detail.attendances.$[a].dogName` (array filter `a.memberId in erased`) in the club's `job_runs`; the erasure step does the same `$unset` for its subject at once. Tests (red first): `ErasureIT`: a `FAILED MemberUpdated` with `diff.phones` and a `job_runs` document with a P3 item naming the erased member's dog → nothing left after `ErasureExecutor.execute` (today both stay); `PrivacyJobsTest`: the sweep deletes a `PUBLISHED` record about an erased member and leaves one about an active member.
3. **S3 (minor) — a support token can schedule an erasure.** `identity/application/SupportAccessService.RESTRICTED:49-53` lists exports, files, `data-export`, `impersonation-token` and `/me` credentials; `platform/api/AuditController.java:86-110` guards `POST`/`DELETE /members/{id}/erasure` with `hasRole('ADMIN')` only, which a support token has (`roles: [ADMIN], support: true`). `@SupportRestricted` guards only `DELETE /support-accesses/{id}`, `/members/{id}/impersonation-token` and two `/me` handlers; every export, file and data-export handler relies on the path regex in `CurrentUserFilter.java:55-57`. Scenario: a support token schedules the irreversible pseudonymisation of a `LEFT` member thirty days out, or cancels one. Rules: S17 R-17-10 (E123), MATRIU «RGPD». Fix: `(POST|DELETE) /api/v1/members/[^/]+/erasure` joins `RESTRICTED` (the `GET` stays readable: the club sees its requests); `@SupportRestricted` on the erasure handlers and on every JWT-guarded handler the regex names (`ExportsController`'s three `*/export`, its `exports/**` and its two `data-export` handlers; `TrainingController:176`, `ActivitiesController:79`, `InstructorController:94`; `RemittancesController:69`; `BillingController:164` — the signed `RemittanceFilesController:27` has no JWT and stays as it is), so each refuses on its own as the Javadoc of `CurrentUserFilter` promises; `SecurityInventoryIT` gains the check that every published operation whose path matches the restricted shapes is both matched by `SupportAccessService.restricted(method, path)` and annotated `@SupportRestricted` (and no annotated handler is outside the regex). The api's copy of S17 R-17-10 and of `MATRIU_PERMISOS.md`'s RGPD row name the erasure routes (E109). Tests (red first): `SupportAccessIT`: `POST /members/{left}/erasure` and `DELETE` with the support token → `403 SUPPORT_RESTRICTED`, `GET` → 200 (today `POST` → 201); `SecurityInventoryIT`'s new assertion red on the unannotated handlers.
4. **S4 (nit) — the booking writers' retry.** `clubs/bookings/application/BookingTransactions.transientConflict:52-57` retries code 112 and `TransientTransactionError` only; `shared/application/TransactionRetries.conflict:48-55` (S05, S06, S09 and `JobRunner.write`) also retries `DuplicateKeyException`/11000 — the first `seat_locks` upsert of a class after the seven-day `seat_lock_ttl` can raise it, and here it would be a 500. With `LocalLanes` on (R1) it cannot happen; the two helpers should still agree. Rule: E5-T07 / R-08-07. Fix: `BookingTransactions` uses `TransactionRetries.conflict` (drop `transientConflict` or make it delegate). Test (red first): `BookingTransactionsSurvivorsTest`: a `DuplicateKeyException` on the first attempt of `write()` → retried and counted (`retries.retried`), today rethrown.
5. **TTL changes never need `collMod`** (INC-82, E11-T09 round-2 verification). `identity/persistence/ImpersonationGrantRepository.java` (≈ 62-63) and `platform/persistence/SecurityEventRepository.java` (≈ 22-23) upgrade an existing TTL index with `IndexOperations.alterIndex` → Mongo's `collMod`, which the production user cannot run: `deploy/mongo-health.js` creates it with `readWrite` only, and `readWrite` has `createIndex`/`dropIndex` but not `collMod` (MongoDB 7 `builtin_roles`). A volume whose index predates the retention value, started under the production user, makes the Core fail at startup (`Unauthorized (13) … collMod`). Replace `alterIndex` by drop + create (`dropIndex(existing name)` then `createIndex(… expire(retention).named(…))`) in both repositories — a few seconds without the TTL index are harmless, the sweeps do not depend on it.
