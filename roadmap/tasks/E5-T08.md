1. **E4-T04 #2** — a FIFO promotion (system) publishes `ActivityRegistrationChanged` with `origin = SYSTEM`, not the origin of the registration it promotes.
2. **E4-T04 #3–#6** — complete the assertions of T-07-15 (`promotedAt`, N-32b `promoted`, cancellation outside the deadline under impersonation → no promotion), T-07-19 (no N-32b for `MEMBER_LEFT`; N-32b for `INACTIVITY`), T-07-14 (N-32b / `ActivityRegistrationChanged` for the `ACTIVE` and the `WAITLISTED` registration), T-07-10 (the 5 `RingBlockCreated` events and `GET /weeks/{id}/calendar` → `activityTitle`), T-07-11 (INSTRUCTOR → 403).

4. **E5-T02 #6** — a same-class swap never raises `ClassBelowMinimum` (the class keeps its count). Test.
5. **E5-T02 #7** — T-08-17 covers its whole spec line.
6. **E5-T02 #8** (also E5-T04 #4) — bookings and training bookings stop bumping `Member.version`: the per-member counters move to a document or field that does not take part in the member's optimistic lock, so an admin editing a member does not get `STALE_VERSION` because the member booked meanwhile. Test that case.
7. **E5-T02 #10** — `cancelFutureByMember(memberId, by, reason)` as in the task contract (S13/S15 will call it).

12. **E5-T03 #4** — PAY_TO_BOOK taking the last seat: the demotion notice (N-46) goes out when the seat is taken, not when the payment succeeds (uses the E5-T02 round 2 flow).
