# E11-T21 · The tolerant CSV reader never loses a valid row

## Objective
E11-T15 round 2 (verified, E162) made the day-D load tolerant of one bad row in `rebuts.csv`, `packs.csv`, `previsio.csv`, `mandats.csv` and `pagat_fins.csv` (`PlayoffInput.ROW_TOLERANT`: a short, long or quote-broken row is `WARNING INPUT_SCHEMA_MISMATCH` on its line and is skipped). The kit's review of that round found that the recovery from an **unterminated quote** can swallow the next valid quoted row: in

```csv
Member ID;Paid until
"broken;2026-12
"valid";2027-01
last;2027-02
```

`PlayoffTable.csvRows`/`readRows` (≈ `:77`) return the header, one `null` and `last` — the row `"valid";2027-01` disappears without a warning, because its opening quote closes the previous row's quote and the recovery then skips past it. In `pagat_fins.csv` that loses a «paid until» override and the December run bills months already paid (S18 R-18-11b, R-18-13). E11-T15's file is full (115 KB), so this is its own short task, thread B, before the gate. **The fix comes with a test that fails before it.**

## Steps
1. **Recovery at the damaged record's boundary.** When a quoted field is not terminated before the end of its line, the reader treats that line as the damaged record (WARNING on its line, skipped) and resumes at the **next line** with a clean state — it never lets the next line's opening quote close the broken one, and never consumes the following records. A quote that is legitimately multi-line (a field with a line break inside quotes, if the files ever carry one) still works when the closing quote arrives on a later line: say in the report how you tell the two apart (the simplest rule: a quoted field may not span more than one line in these files — then say so in S18 R-18-11b and DEPLOY.md). Unit test on `PlayoffTable` (red today): the four-line file above → rows `[header, null (line 2), ["valid","2027-01"], ["last","2027-02"]]` and the warning names line 2; a second case with the damaged row last; a third with a stray `"` inside an unquoted value (`a;b"c;d` → the row kept or skipped as the current rule says, never the next row lost).

### Summary
- **Step 1, the fix** (`PlayoffTable.parse`, lenient mode only). In `csvRows`/`readRows` a quoted field may not span lines: a line break (`\n` or `\r`) met while a quote is open makes **that line** the damaged record (`null` → `WARNING INPUT_SCHEMA_MISMATCH` on its line, from `PlayoffInput`). The reader then resumes at the next line with a clean state (`quoted`/`closed`/fields reset; `nextLine` handles CRLF). So the next line's opening quote can no longer close the broken one. A quote still open at the end of the text breaks only the last line. Since a record can no longer span lines, the old "re-read from the line after the opening quote" recovery (`opened`) is gone. Line numbers stay exact: one row of the table = one line, and `PlayoffInput`'s `i+1` is the line.
- **How I tell a legitimate multi-line field from a broken quote:** I don't try to guess. In the five row-tolerant files (`rebuts.csv`, `packs.csv`, `previsio.csv`, `mandats.csv`, `pagat_fins.csv`) a quoted field never spans lines. That is the simplest rule the task allows, and none of their columns (ids, dates, numbers, amounts, statuses, a concept) needs a line break. The **strict** reader (`csv`/`read`, census files and every other caller) is unchanged and still accepts a quoted line break (the new unit test asserts both). Written in S18 R-18-11b (h) (+ history line) and in `docs/DEPLOY.md` (the F10 runbook list, after the E156 line).
