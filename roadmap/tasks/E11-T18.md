# E11-T18 · The template club's catalogs

## Objective
S17 R-17-13 and T-17-14 say the template club `club-template-default` carries catalogs and message templates in ca/es/en, and D19's «Catàlegs base» step clones the parts the platform admin ticks (`POST /platform/clubs/{id}/catalog-clone {fromClubId, parts: [LEVELS, RINGS, PLANS, FAQ, MESSAGE_TEMPLATES]}`, R-17-08). Today the seed has `levels: []`, `rings: []`, `plans: []`, `faq: []`, `messageTemplates: []`, so a new club clones nothing. Jordi (21-10, ruling E153): the usual case is a club that starts from a generic set and then adapts it, so the starter set must be there and easy to add — part by part, editable afterwards. No change to the clone API or the wizard: the seed is the whole task.

## Spec references
- S17 §2 (D19 «Catàlegs base», the wizard's step 6), R-17-08 (clone, parts, `CATALOG_NOT_EMPTY`), R-17-13 (ca/es/en), T-17-07, T-17-14.
- S05 (levels, rings, plans, prices, FAQ), S11 §3 (message templates per notification code: N-01…N-54 of `CATALEG_NOTIFICACIONS.md`, the per-template channel × audience matrix), `MODEL_DADES_PLATAFORMA.md` §0 (English code names), `seeds/club-definition.schema.json`.

## Context to load
- `seeds/club-template-default.yaml`, `seeds/club-canic.yaml` (the shape of every catalog row and of the 52 message templates; copy the structure, never the club's own texts where they name the club), `seeds/README.md`, `club:apply` (`PlatformDefinitionSchema`, `ClubDefinitionCodec`), `platform/application/PlatformCatalogClone.java`, the seed tests (`SeedsIT`/`ClubApplyIT` or as named), `E10` smoke `bin/e10-smoke` (it creates a second club and clones).
- `docs/DECISIONS_COPY_A39-A41.md` (in the repo since 21-10): A41.1 (the starter set) and A40.4 (the error messages).

## Steps
1. **Levels** (`levels`): Cadells · Iniciació · Intermedi · Avançat · Teràpia · Pendent — ca/es/en (`Cachorros / Puppies`, `Iniciación / Beginners`, `Intermedio / Intermediate`, `Avanzado / Advanced`, `Terapia / Therapy`, `Pendiente / Pending`), with the same `progression`/`canTrainAlone` semantics as the Cànic's rows (E29: «i sup.» progression where the Cànic has it; the template's `PENDENT` level as the Cànic's). Test: the seed applies and `GET /levels` on the template club lists the six, each `LocalizedText` complete in the three locales.
2. **Rings** (`rings`): «Pista 1» and «Pista 2» (`Pista 1 / Ring 1`, `Pista 2 / Ring 2`), free training allowed on both, no geometry (S16 is per club). Test as in step 1.
3. **Plans and prices** (`plans`, `prices`): the generic plan types — a MONTHLY membership («Abonament mensual / Abono mensual / Monthly membership»), a PACK of 10 sessions («Pack de 10 sessions / Bono de 10 sesiones / 10-session pack»; es says «Bono», ruling E153/A41), a SINGLE_CLASS («Classe solta / Clase suelta / Single class»), and a MONTHLY plan with `billingMode = MAINTENANCE` («Teràpia / Terapia / Therapy»), plus the entry fee and the additional-dog fee concepts — with the Cànic's current amounts as the starter prices (a club edits them in D8), currency EUR, tax 0 (`taxPercent` kept). Test: the seed applies; `PriceResolver` resolves every concept on today's date; `catalog-clone` with `PLANS` into an empty EUR club copies them and a non-EUR club gets `422 CURRENCY_MISMATCH` (R-17-08, existing test or a new one).
4. **FAQ** (`faq`): the three categories of the Cànic («Reserves», «Pagaments», «El club» — or the three the Cànic seed uses: take its category names) with one placeholder entry each whose answer is the R-05-21b marker («[Text pendent — el club l'omplirà des de Paràmetres]» in the three locales), so the clone yields a visible skeleton the club fills from D11. Test: three categories, each with one entry, `published: false` if the model supports it (else say so).
5. **Message templates** (`messageTemplates`): all 52 templates of the Cànic seed, in ca/es/en, with the club's name taken from the `{club}` variable (never a literal), the same channel × audience matrix as the catalog's defaults, and no text that names the Cànic, its rings, its times (`07:30`) or its town. Test: the template club has exactly the catalog's codes (parity with `CATALEG_NOTIFICACIONS.md` — reuse the parity test of E7-T01 if it is club-agnostic, or add one), every `LocalizedText` complete in ca/es/en, and `grep -i canic|cànic|7:30|agilitycanic` over the template seed finds nothing.
6. **Seeds twice and the clone**: `bin/core club:apply seeds/club-template-default.yaml` twice (idempotent, no diff on the second), then `bin/e10-smoke` (the second club clones from the template): the cloned club has the five parts; `CATALOG_NOT_EMPTY` on a second clone. `seeds/README.md` describes the starter set and that a club edits everything after cloning.
7. **The api's error messages in tu/tú** (ruling E153, A40.4). The ICU messages of `src/main/resources/messages/messages_{ca,es}.properties` for the error codes that the web lists in «vosaltres»/«usted» (26 ca: Introduïu, teniu, Inicieu, Completeu, Accepteu, Activeu, Confirmeu, Reviseu, Actualitzeu…; 26 es: Introduzca, Complete, Acepte, Active, Confirme, Revise, Inicie, Actualícelo, No puede, No tiene, su propio…) move to tu/tú, the same wording the web adopts in E11-W05 (the before/after table is `docs/DECISIONS_COPY_A39-A41.md` §A40.4, in the repo; keep placeholders). `CATALEG_ERRORS.md`'s texts, where it quotes them, follow (repo copy, E109). Test: a unit test over the two properties files asserts none of the listed second-person-plural/formal forms remains for those codes (red before the change).
8. **Evidence.** `./mvnw -q clean verify` under the host lock; `bin/e10-smoke` green; the two `club:apply` runs.

Do not edit the clone API, the wizard or the Cànic seed. If a row needs a product decision the rulings do not give (a price, a level's rule), write the assumption in the report and go on.
