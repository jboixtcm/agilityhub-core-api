## Objective
Two of the ten scheduled processes become real `Job` beans on E5-T01's framework — `no-show-notices` (P3), which every morning turns yesterday's «no presentat» marks into a single batch of N-19, and `class-finishing` (P8), which every minute closes the classes whose grace period has run out and silently sweeps the waiting entries of classes that have already started. ## Steps
1. **`NoShowNoticesJob`** (`clubs/bookings/application/jobs`, registered as a `Job` bean, `JobName.NO_SHOW_NOTICES`, route id `no-show-notices`) — R-15-13 + R-10-06. `plan(ctx)`: `AttendanceRepository.claimForNoShowNotice`'s **read-only twin** — `state = NO_SHOW ∧ noShowNotice.queuedAt = null ∧ classDate < ctx.localDate` — returning one `JobItem` per attendance with `{attendanceId, bookingId, memberId, dogName, classDate}`; in dry run they surface as `WOULD_NOTIFY`. `apply`: **one transaction** that claims the whole set with `claimForNoShowNotice` (writing `noShowNotice.queuedAt = now` and the shared `eventId`) and puts a **single** `NoShowNoticeDue {attendanceIds[], bookingIds[]}` on the outbox; the dispatcher then renders **one N-19 per booking** to the dog's owner (APP+EMAIL subject to the member's `PERSONAL` preferences) with `dog_name` and `class_date` — the class's own date, never «ahir». Counters `{notices, late}` where `late` counts classes older than one day (a mark added two days after the class). Running the job twice, or manually right after the scheduled run, claims nothing and emits nothing (R-15-04). A mark changed to `PRESENT`/`NOTIFIED` **before** the batch is never notified; changed **after**, the notice is already out and the sheet shows «avís ja enviat» — which is the `NotificationSent{N-19}` consumer of S10 §7 writing `noShowNotice.sentAt` (implement it here if E6-T02 left it out; say which). Catch-up **unlimited** (the scope is by state, so a server that was down for three days notifies everything in one late run). No module guard. **T-15-16, T-10-26**.
2. **`ClassFinishingJob`** (`clubs/bookings/application/jobs`, `JobName.CLASS_FINISHING`, route id `class-finishing`, cadence CONTINUOUS) — R-15-18, two steps in this order: **(a)** `WaitlistService.sweepStarted(now)` → every `WaitlistEntry` `ACTIVE` or `NOTIFIED` whose class has `startsAt ≤ now` becomes `CANCELLED {cancelReason: CLASS_STARTED}` **silently** (no notification, S08 R-08-16); **(b)** `ClassSessionService.finishEnded(now)` → every `ClassSession` `ACTIVE` with `endsAt + classes.finishGraceMinutes (15) ≤ now` becomes `FINISHED` with `finishedAt`, over the index `{clubId, state, endsAt}`. **No business event** — only the framework's `SchedulerRun`. Idempotent by state. Dry run: `WOULD_SWEEP {entryId}` and `WOULD_FINISH {classId}` with no write anywhere. No rollback (`FINISHED` is terminal in S06). After finishing, only `notes` stays editable on the class and the attendance sheet keeps working until `T1` (R-10-03) — assert both. Example to encode: a class 18:50–19:50 is `FINISHED` on the 20:05 tick. **T-15-26**.

### Assumptions
1. **P3 as one transaction on the per-item runner.** The plan lists one item per attendance. The first item still in scope claims the whole set with `NoShowNoticeClaims.claim` inside its item transaction: one `NoShowNoticeDue`, the batch counters `{notices, late}`. Later items find their row queued by that batch and are only traced, with no counters. An item that left the scope is `NOT_IN_SCOPE`: gone, changed to PRESENT, a class of the run's day, or claimed elsewhere. The job lease keeps any other run out. Only the CLI `attendance:claim-no-show` does not take it.
2. **`late`** = the class date is before the run's date minus one day, i.e. a class older than yesterday.
3. **N-19 and `sentAt`:**
   - The N-19 dispatcher is the consumer of `NoShowNoticeDue`. Its ids are `eventId:bookingId:app|email`.
   - It writes `noShowNotice.sentAt` once a channel of the booking is SENT. S10 §7's `NotificationSent{N-19}` consumer is folded into it, because an APP row is SENT on insertion and publishes no `NotificationSent` before E7.
   - PERSONAL preferences do not exist before E7-T03, so the email always goes, as for N-20.
   - A mark changed after the batch still gets its notice.
4. **`class_date`** is the class's club-local date, written out in full in the recipient's language («dilluns, 5 d’octubre de 2026»). `class_description` is not sent (see proposal 2).
5. **P8 `now`** is the tick's minute (`scheduledFor`), for both the sweep and the finish. The cutoff is `now − classes.finishGraceMinutes`, recorded in `parametersSnapshot`. Step (c) uses S07's own rule (`endsAt < now`, no grace) and keeps S07's `ActivityFinished`. «No business event» applies to classes.
6. **Club cancellation text:** `ClassCancellationUseCase` now hands `adminText` to S08 through a 4-argument port default. The booking's `cancelMessage` then carries it: the history's `detail.message` and `GET /bookings/{id}` `cancellation.message`.
7. **Demo seed:**
   - Five steps write it, each in its own context, because of the ArchUnit rules: bookings ⊬ followup, contexts only through `application`, no cross-context persistence types. The steps are the census cast (36), the S06 past classes (inside the planning step, 10), the S08/S10 bookings and marks (37), the follow-up tasks and observation (38) and the S09 trainings (41).
   - Real services only.
   - «As of» instants for bookings, marks, cancellations, the P3 claim and trainings. Tasks, notes and observations keep the real clock, so they are dated at seed time.
8. **The cast:**
   - Laura's role is played by the login `member.8@example.test` (census ordinal 12), whose dogs are CAD and E. Her CAD dog moves up to A through `DogService.level` (`DogLevelChanged`, N-09), so she fits the A+B class.
   - The others are single-dog B members (ordinals 70/75/80/85).
   - Alba's and Nil's histories are read through the admin's impersonation token, because non-login demo accounts have no password.
9. **The instructor** is renamed through `RoleAssignmentService.renameInstructor` (a small new method that keeps colour and state) and `MemberService.patch`. Which seed login it is depends on the club id, so the smoke finds it by `shortName`.
10. **The run book** is the root `README.md` plus `docs/DEPLOY.md`.
