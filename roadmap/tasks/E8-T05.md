# E8-T05 · Packs, inactivity and leave (S12 WP-12-E · S13 WP-13-B · WP-13-C)

## Objective
Work packages S12 WP-12-E, S13 WP-13-B and S13 WP-13-C. Three things that all end up on the same receipt: a **pack** that opens on payment, is consumed and refunded by bookings, warns when it runs low and loses its remaining sessions at expiry; an **inactivity period** a member asks for under the day-25 rule, that the admin approves (cancelling the bookings inside it), that starts and ends by itself and that replaces the monthly fee with the inactivity fee; and a **leave** the member or the admin schedules, that fixes the last invoiced month, cancels later bookings and turns the member into `LEFT` the day after. The same task replaces every double the earlier stages left behind: `PackBalancePort` and `InactivityPort` of E5-T02 (`clubs/bookings/application/ports`) and `InactivityFeePort`, `LeaveBillingPort` and `PackBalanceOpeningPort` of E8-T02 (`payments/application/ports`). The two schedulers become **jobs** in E8-T06; here they are plain services with an explicit `executeDue(clubId, today)` entry point.

## Fixed conventions
- Statuses (catalog first, **rule 0 overrides what S13 §6 writes**): `INACTIVITY_OVERLAP` **409** (suffix `_OVERLAP`) · `INACTIVITY_DEADLINE_PASSED`, `INACTIVITY_INVALID_RANGE`, `INACTIVITY_INVALID_STATE`, `INACTIVITY_NOT_APPLICABLE`, `LEAVE_ALREADY_REQUESTED`, `LEAVE_ALREADY_SCHEDULED`, `LEAVE_INVALID_STATE`, `LEAVE_DATE_INVALID`, `LEAVE_REASON_UNKNOWN`, `MEMBER_NOT_LEFT`, `NO_PLANNED_LEAVE`, `MEMBER_NOT_ACTIVE`, `INACTIVITY_PERIOD`, `MEMBER_LEAVING`, `PACK_NEGATIVE`, `PLAN_NOT_PACK`, `AMOUNT_EXCEEDS_DUE` **422** (only the bare `ALREADY_` prefix makes a code 409 — confirmed 09-09) · `STALE_VERSION` **409** · `READ_ONLY`, `IMPERSONATION_DENIED` **403** · `MODULE_DISABLED`, `NOT_FOUND` **404** · `VALIDATION_ERROR` **400**.
