## Steps
1. **An accepted send is never sent again, whatever happens to its settlement (review #1, R-11-09; E7-T02's round-2 assumption 2).**
   - Today, after five failed settlements the accepted outcome lives only in the dispatcher's memory (`unsettled`), and the delivery's two-minute lease is not renewed. After the lease expires, another dispatcher claims the delivery and sends it again; so does the same one after a restart.
   - Record the acceptance durably before anything else can claim the delivery. For example: write `providerRef` and an «accepted» mark on the claimed delivery in its own small update (retried), make `claimDue` skip a delivery that carries it, and let the settlement complete it later. Or renew the lease while the settlement is retried. Whatever the design, a delivery that the provider accepted never reaches the provider again.
   - Tests: the settlement fails until the lease expires, then a second dispatcher polls → no second provider call, and the delivery ends `SENT` once the database answers. The same after the dispatcher is replaced (a new bean, with empty memory).
2. **Bounce suppression at every e-mail attempt (review #2, R-11-08).**
   - Before each e-mail attempt, retries included, check the address against both marks with `EmailSuppression` (the contact's `bounced` and `Account.emailStatus`).
   - A suppressed delivery ends `SKIPPED_NO_CONTACT`, as one resolved without an address: no provider call, and no `NotificationSent` or `NotificationFailed`.
   - Test: two e-mails queued to one address; the first hard-bounces (webhook); the second, and a pending retry to that address, end skipped without a provider call.
3. **A push subscription's expiry is never lost (review #3, R-11-07).**
   - On `GONE`, the subscription's `EXPIRED` and its `PushUnsubscribed` event belong to the retried settlement, which keeps the provider's answer. Nothing swallows their failure.
   - Test: inject a failure of that transaction (its outbox write) → no second push; eventually the subscription is `EXPIRED`, with exactly one `PushUnsubscribed`.
4. **The monthly SMS counter never goes back (review #4, R-11-06).**
   - A sender that computed the old month but reaches `reserve()` after another one started the new month must not overwrite the new month's count. The same holds for `firstCapNotice()`.
   - Store the counters and the notice markers per month (for example under `{clubId, month}` keys), or make the rollover move only forward.
   - Tests: a delayed old-month `reserve` and `firstCapNotice` after the new month began → the new month's count and marker are intact, and the cap still holds.
5. **The e-mail routes are the web's (review #5, R-11-11; answer to E7-T02's round-2 question 1).**
   - Compare every route of `NotificationLinks` with the web's two route lists; where the spec and the web differ, the web's route wins, because it is what people open. List each change in the report.
   - Known: training is `/entrenaments` (screen 08) and `/entrenaments/{trainingBookingId}` (its detail), never `/training`; `OPEN_SETUP` must not open training; `OPEN_JOBS` (N-42) is `/parametres#processos` in the back office (S15 §2), not `/tauler`; `/rebuts` stays until E8 builds the receipts screen.
   - Correct `NotificationLinksTest`.
6. **The dispatcher's failure-path table.**
   - In the report, list every write of the dispatch path: the claim, the provider call, the settlement, the events, the cap bookkeeping, the push bookkeeping and the webhook's moves.
   - For each, say what happens when it fails, or when the process stops right after it: the end state, whether anything can be sent twice or lost, and the test that proves it.
   - Fix any other row that can send twice or lose a send, with its test.

### Assumptions
1. **The durable mark (step 1)** is a new internal field, `deliveries[].acceptedAt`. Like `claimToken`/`claimedUntil`, the
   API never reads it: no contract change (the snapshot is identical). The settlement removes it, so a partial index holds
   only the pending ones.
2. **A sender late on the old month (step 4)** counts in the stored later month: its SMS goes out now. If it meets the cap
   there, its outcome names that month (`SmsCapReached{2026-11}`). An old month's notice is dropped once the marker is on
   a later month: that month is over, and the marker keeps the current month's single N-49.
3. **A suppressed e-mail (step 2)** is `SKIPPED_NO_CONTACT` with `lastError` «Email address suppressed». `attempts` is
   unchanged, and `target` stays, so the log shows which address was stopped. A recipient without a member contact (an
   applicant, an admin without a member record) is checked against the club's members that have the address, the set the
   webhook marks, and against its account's mark.
4. **`sentAt`** of a delivery settled later is the acceptance's time, not the settlement's.
5. **`PROVIDER_WINDOW`** (30 s) is a code constant, like `LEASE`: technical, not a club rule, so no parameter. The providers'
   own bound is 15 s (5 s to connect + 10 s for the answer, `EmailConfiguration`/`MessagingConfiguration`).
6. **After 5 failed settlements, a push answer (`GONE`/`FAILED`/`OK`) waits in memory** like an acceptance. After a
   restart, only an `OK` is durable (row 7); a `GONE`/`FAILED` is asked again, and the push service answers the same.
