## Objective
Work packages S07 P2 and P3: the club maintains activities in D7, publishes them (rings blocked synchronously through E4-T03's `RingBlockService.syncForActivity`, with the conflict dialog that may cancel classes), cancels them with N-32c; a member registers per person (no dog) with automatic FIFO waitlist promotion; the rows of 04/03/25 and the grid cell come from `ActivityQueryService`; the club website reads upcoming activities through the public API without personal data; every S07 route replaces its E4-T01 stub.
