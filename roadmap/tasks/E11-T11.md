## Steps
1. **The files of an erasure that does not complete** (E11-T01 review #1, major).
   - When an `ACCOUNT` request is cancelled after some clubs were erased, those clubs' files still leave S3: `ErasureFilesCleanup.clean` and the daily retry also process a `CANCELLED` request with `filesPending`, without moving it to `ERASED`. Or publish a cleanup per club when its part runs.
   - The same applies while a request waits or is `BLOCKED` between clubs: an erased club's files do not wait for the whole request.
   - The IT gives club A a dog photo, a document and a task attachment, cancels the request halfway, and asserts that the files have left the store.
2. **Another member's names stay out of `data.json`** (E11-T01 review #2). `familyGroupClaim` keeps only `status` and `holderMemberId` in the package: the holder's name and the holder's dog are another member's data (R-14-13, «sense dades d'altres membres»). Adjust `MemberDataPackageTest`.
3. **The instructor's old name in the outbox** (E11-T01 review #3). The erasure also deletes the processed `InstructorChanged` records of the renamed instructor (by `aggregateId`). Test it with an instructor written through `InstructorWriter`, not inserted directly.
4. **The fail-closed filter** (E11-T01 review #5 and #6): keys ending in `names` are covered too, and the actor flag carries into maps nested inside a `…By` map.
5. **S14 §6's «Idempotent» column** (E11-T01 review #7). Check what the column means in `CONVENCIONS_API.md`, and make the rows of `DELETE /platform/erasure-requests/{id}` and `DELETE /members/{id}/erasure` say the same and the right thing.
6. **The grace's trade-off in the spec** (E11-T02 review #1). R-01-06 says it plainly: a stolen token replayed within the grace keeps an independent branch of the family until it forks again or replays a spent token. That is the price of not logging out a user whose two tabs refresh at the same time (INC-07). The organizer accepted it at E134.
7. **The sessions list** (E11-T02 review #2). `families()` keeps the family's token with the latest `lastUsedAt`, with a test of which token `GET /me/sessions` lists.
8. **An atomic chain guard** (E11-T02 review #3). The grace's check that the child has not moved on is a conditional write on the child, inside the grace's transaction (for example `replacedByHash: null` → set a `graceSiblingAt`), so a concurrent rotation of the child cannot slip between the read and the sibling. Test it with the two calls interleaved.
9. **`BankAccountVault` without its key** (E11-T02 review #4):
   - the refusal is an `ApiException(INTERNAL_ERROR)` with the cause (AGENTS rule 9);
   - the application fails at startup outside `local` and `test` when `BILLING_BANK_KEY` is missing;
   - the permissive constructor is for tests only.
10. **`VenueRepository.freeSlug`** (E11-T02 review #5) returns the slug the club's venue really has, or nothing.

Each fix comes with a test that fails before it (for the spec, the diff). Do not edit `docs/INCIDENCIES_OBERTES.md`.
