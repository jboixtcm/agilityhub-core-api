   - P9 `cleanup` reports the expired documents that the TTL has not removed yet, in `ttlPending.ringSlotLocks`, like the other TTL collections.
   - Tests: the index exists with its options after startup; an upsert sets `expiresAt`; the P9 counter counts an expired document.
