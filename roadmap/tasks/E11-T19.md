# E11-T19 · The console's entry on the product host, the brand preset, and the api minors of 21-10

## Objective
E10-W02's integration against the real core found that **nobody can sign in to the console on the product host**: `POST /oauth2/token` with `Host: clubsadmin.<product>` answers `404 UNKNOWN_HOST` for every client and grant (S01 R-01-02 «host desconegut → 404» runs before any platform-host rule), while `/branding` and bearer `/api/v1/*` already treat that host as a platform host (E10-T04). S17 §2 «Entrada» says the console admits only accounts with `AGILITYHUB_ADMIN`, with a token without club context. The ID's `id-web` session cannot travel to the console host (host-only cookie, client-bound refresh, the handoff needs a club context). Ruling E157: the product host issues `clubs-admin` tokens to platform admins. Then the product branding preset takes the brand's values, and the minors of the 21-10 verifications of E11-T13, E11-T15 and E10-W02 are closed.

**Each fix comes with a test that fails before it.**
