## Steps
1. **`bin/e3-smoke` green again (E3-T07).**
   - The «Future verticals must stay empty» assertion (`bin/e3-smoke:238-239`) is older than the E4-T05 planning seed. Replace it with the S14 R-14-03 expectation for the seeded current week:
     - `capacity` = Σ `capacity` of the week's `ACTIVE`/`FINISHED` classes;
     - `booked` = Σ `counters.booked`;
     - `percent` = round(100·booked/capacity), or `null` when the capacity is 0.
   - Compute the expected values from what the smoke itself reads (the week's class sessions, or the seed), never as literals.
   - `trainingBookings` stays `{0, 0}` while the seed has no training bookings.
   - Keep every other assertion.
2. **M6 · resolved texts in `GET /signup`.** `texts.paymentDay` and `texts.familyGroupIntro` still carry `{deadlineDay}` and `{twoDogsMonthlyFee}` (`SignupService.java:221-237`; the E3-W03 screenshots 18/19 show them). Interpolate on the server, per locale:
   - `{deadlineDay}` = the day that S13 returns as `deadlineDay` in `GET /me/inactivity-periods` (25 at the Cànic); read the same parameter.
   - `{twoDogsMonthlyFee}` = the current monthly price of the family-fare plan that R-04-13 proposes (the eligible `Plan` with `dogsIncluded` = 2, resolved as `SignupPolicy` already does), formatted as money in the text's locale.
   - A club without a family-fare plan gets `familyGroupIntro: null`, never a raw placeholder.
   
   IT: for the Cànic seed and for the minimal club, in ca, es and en, no value in `texts` contains `{` or `}`.
3. **M7 · payment instructions (§2 row 19, §10, N-01).**
   - `paymentMethods[MANUAL].instructions` = `CLUB.paymentProviders.MANUAL.instructions` (model §2), not `signup.text.cashConditions` (`SignupService.java:226`).
   - The cash conditions stay in `texts.cashConditions`. The web shows them only under the CASH method (E3-W06).
   - The applicant's N-01 (public signup and add-dog) renders `upfront_total` and `payment_instructions`, from the frozen `signup.upfront.totalDue` and the chosen method's instructions.
   - `pay_link` belongs to Stripe (**E8-T04**); leave it empty here.
   
   Tests:
   - `GET /signup` returns the club's MANUAL instructions;
   - the ca N-01 body carries the total and the instructions.
4. **M5, api side · a quote per plan in `GET /signup` (R-04-14/15).**
   - Today the config builds one set of first-month options from the first monthly plan, and the web shows it for every plan.
   - Return a quote **per offered plan**, computed with the same code as the submission: its `lines` (`ENTRY_FEE` only if > 0, `PACK`, …) and, for `MONTHLY` plans with a standard monthly price, the two first-month options. Each option is `{option: TODAY | ALTERNATIVE, portion: FULL | HALF, startDate, amountDue, totalDue}`, with `totalDue` = the lines + that option. `PACK` and `SINGLE_CLASS` plans have a plan-level `totalDue` and no options.
   - Add-dog mode: the TODAY option (entry fee + `ADDITIONAL_DOG_FEE` of the current month) **always** exists. The ALTERNATIVE option («alta l'1 de {mes següent}», entry fee only) exists only while today ≤ `billing.upfrontCutoffDay`. Today `additionalDogOptions` is empty after the cutoff, so the add-dog quote has no payable line.
   
   Tests, on the R-04-14/15 examples:
   - an ABONAT quote on 17-08-2026: 100 + 30 = 130 €, and 100 + 60 €;
   - on 05-08: TODAY FULL and ALTERNATIVE HALF;
   - Pack 6 → 135 €;
   - Teràpia → 50 €, with no options;
   - add-dog on day 26 → TODAY only.
5. **M8 · assignable plans are not only the plans offered at signup.**
   - `SignupPlanCatalog` (`:18`) filters `active ∧ showOnSignup ∧ module`, and every use goes through it. The Cànic's `ABONAT_FAMILIAR` has `showOnSignup: false`.
   - Keep that list only for the public offer: the `GET /signup` plans and the applicant's `planIdRequested`.
   - Everything else uses the **assignable** plans (`active ∧ module`, whatever `showOnSignup` says):
     - the family-fare proposal (`proposedPlan`, R-04-13);
     - the admin's `planId`/`priceId` at validation and in `dryRun`;
     - add-dog (`addDog` → the member's current plan, and `additionalFee`);
     - loading D2 when the requested plan was hidden afterwards.
   - Add `planOptions[]` to `GET /members/{id}/signup`: the assignable plans, each with `{planId, name (resolved), type, prices[{priceId, amount, periodicity, concept}]}`. The D2 plan selector (E3-W07) reads it.
   
   Tests:
   - a FOUND family claim → the proposal is `ABONAT_FAMILIAR`, and validation with it succeeds;
   - a member on `ABONAT_FAMILIAR` adds a dog (201), and `GET /signup` works for that member on days 1–25;
   - D2 still loads after the requested plan gets `showOnSignup: false`;
   - a public `POST /signup` with a hidden plan → `422 PLAN_NOT_AVAILABLE` (unchanged).
6. **M9 · the admins' N-01 (§8, N-01).** The admins get the applicant's text today («Hem rebut la teva sol·licitud…», `SignupNotifications.java:34`).
   - Add admin keys in ca, es and en that use `member_name`, `dogs` and `plan_name`, with the D2 link (`OPEN_SIGNUP`).
   - The applicant's copy never carries `OPEN_SIGNUP`.
   
   Test: both bodies asserted in ca, for a public signup and for an add-dog.
7. **M10 · upfront rows belong to one submission, and amounts never change (§5, ruling E39).**
   - Each `UpfrontPayment` row carries `submissionId`: a new id per `POST /signup` or `POST /me/dogs/signup`, also kept in `Member.signup`. Every read of «the rows of this signup» filters by it: D2, validation, `dryRun`, rejection and checkout. The rows of an earlier submission (a readmission, R-04-06/07) are history and never count.
   - `replace()` (`UpfrontPayments.java:63-79`) handles a plan change at D2:
     - it cancels only `DUE` rows and creates the new ones;
     - it never rewrites `amountDue` or `amountPaid` of an existing row;
     - `PAID` and `PARTIAL` rows stay as they are, and their `amountPaid` is deducted from the new amount due.
   - **E39:** a plan change while a row of the submission is `CHECKOUT_PENDING` is refused with `409 INVALID_STATE`, `details.reason = CHECKOUT_PENDING`. `dryRun` returns the same condition as a warning.
   - Propose `submissionId` for `MODEL_DADES_CANIC.md` in the report.
   
   Tests:
   - a readmitted member whose earlier application has a `PAID` entry fee for the same dog: D2 and validation see only the new rows;
   - a plan change with a `PARTIAL` row keeps that row intact and deducts it;
   - a plan change with a `CHECKOUT_PENDING` row → 409.
8. **M11 · D1 fresh right after a command (R-14-01).** The dashboard and menu-counter caches are evicted only by the async outbox consumers (`DashboardEvents`, polled every second), so D1 still lists a signup that was just validated.
   - Evict synchronously after commit in the commands that change the pending list or the KPIs:
     - submission and add-dog;
     - validation and rejection;
     - the D2 edits that change a pending row.
   - Keep the async path for the other sources.
   - `GET /members/{id}/signup` also returns `warnDays` (`dashboard.pendingSignupAgeWarnDays`), so D2 no longer calls `/dashboard`.
   - A member with nothing pending answers `409 INVALID_STATE` with `details.reason = NOT_PENDING`; D2 shows a clear message (E3-W07).
   
   IT, without any sleep: validate → `GET /dashboard` and `GET /dashboard/counters` straight away → the signup is gone and the counts are updated. The same for a rejection and for a submission.
9. **M20, api side · pending dogs on screen 13 (ruling E36).**
   - `GET /me/dogs` also returns the member's own `PENDING` dogs, and every dog carries `status` (`ACTIVE` | `PENDING`).
   - `ACTIVE` dogs keep everything they have today. A `PENDING` dog carries only `{id, name, breed, sex, ageYears, status}`: no tasks, documents, notes or pack.
   - Screen 13 is the only user of this route. The S08/S09 selectors read `/me/family-group`, which does not change.
   
   IT:
   - after `POST /me/dogs/signup`, `GET /me/dogs` lists the new dog with `status: PENDING`;
   - after validation, the dog is `ACTIVE`;
   - after a rejection, it is gone.
10. **M21 · the §3 constraints, enforced by the server.**
    - `nextInvoiceDate` ≥ `signup.upfront.firstMonth.startDate`, at validation, in `dryRun` and in the D2 `PATCH` → `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` on that field.
    - `birthDate` in the past and ≥ 1900-01-01; `SignupService.java:141` checks only the future.
    - `chip` per country profile (§3 `Dog`):
      - `ES` → 15 digits; `GENERIC` → 8–15 alphanumerics;
      - normalised (spaces and dashes removed, upper case) **before** the uniqueness check (R-04-07) and the readmission match;
      - enforced in `POST /signup`, `POST /me/dogs/signup` and the D2 dog `PATCH`.
    
    Tests per rule. Include a chip typed with spaces that equals an existing chip → `422 DOG_CHIP_ALREADY_REGISTERED`.
11. **The dog's own version in D2 (M12, api side).** Each dog of `GET /members/{id}/signup` carries its `version`: the one that `PATCH /dogs/{id}` compares (`DogService.java:34`). Test: after an add-dog and a member edit, the dog `PATCH` with the dog's version answers 200.
12. **The signup flags in `GET /signup`.**
    - Expose `allowFamilyGroupPending` (`signup.allowFamilyGroupPending`, module `FAMILY_GROUP`) and `requireDogDocumentAtSignup` (`signup.requireDogDocumentAtSignup`), so the web can enforce them (E3-W08).
    - The server keeps enforcing both at submission.
    
    Contract test.
13. **E35 · D1 «Gossos per nivell».**
    - The chart uses the progression levels, like D3 coverage (R-06-06 after E29): one column per `Level.active ∧ progression`, in `order`.
    - The dogs of every other level (Teràpia, Pendent, inactive levels) count in `others` and in `totalActiveDogs`.
    - Update T-14-06 and the smoke's seed expectation.
