### Assumptions
1. **N-01 copies.** The copies are message variants of the same catalog code: `notif.N-01.admin.*` for the admins, and `notif.N-01.upfront.*` for an applicant with an amount due. The notification row keeps `N-01`. I did not use an `audience` variable, because the catalog row of N-01 does not list one.
   - The admin email has no URL. The D2 link is the APP row's `action: OPEN_SIGNUP` + `entityId`.
   - The applicant's copies (EMAIL, and the member's APP row in add-dog) carry neither.
2. **`payment_instructions`.** It is `MANUAL.instructions` whenever no provider checkout is required, whatever the monthly method, because the upfront is paid to the club (§2 row 19). With a checkout it is empty, and `pay_link` stays empty (E8-T04).
3. **`{deadlineDay}` and the family intro.**
   - `{deadlineDay}` comes from `inactivity.requestDeadlineDay`, the parameter behind S13's `deadlineDay`. `GET /me/inactivity-periods` is not implemented yet.
   - Without BILLING there is no price, so there is no family fare, and `familyGroupIntro` is `null`.
4. **The kept rows of a plan change (E39).** The new rows are the new quote minus what the kept `PAID`/`PARTIAL` rows already charge (`amountDue`). No existing amount changes, and the outstanding total is «new quote − paid». Edge case: see question 1.
5. **Family fare for groups of 3 or more dogs.** When the group has more dogs than any plan includes, D2 proposes the largest family fare. R-04-13 says «≥ 2 dogs → the family fare if it exists»; the first smoke attempt hit this case (log `05a`). The add-dog fee keeps the strict rule: it falls back to `billing.familyDiscountPercentFromSecondDog`.
6. **`nextInvoiceDate` in «the D2 PATCH».** `PATCH /members/{id}` does not accept `nextInvoiceDate`: its allow-list answers 400. So the constraint is enforced at validation and in `dryRun`, where the field exists.
7. **`birthDate` «in the past».** It means strictly before the club's today. The same rule now applies to the S03 member PATCH.
8. **The chip format in PATCH `/dogs/{id}`.** It is enforced for `PENDING` dogs only (the D2 PATCH). An `ACTIVE` dog keeps the S03 contract: `CensusIT` expects `CHIP_ALREADY_EXISTS` with a 6-digit chip, and migrated chips may not match. Uniqueness on PATCH stays with the `dog_chip` index (`409 CHIP_ALREADY_EXISTS`).
9. **Add-dog.**
   - The member's own plan is assignable even when hidden.
