## Steps
1. **M16 · the signed upload headers (R-04-08).**
   - `SignupController.uploadUrl` (`:146`) returns `UploadUrl(uploadUrl, fileKey, expiresAt)`. It drops the `headers` that `AttachmentService.signupUpload` returns: `Content-Type` and `If-None-Match: *`, both signed by `S3AttachmentStorage`.
   - Return `headers` (a map) in the signup and add-dog upload responses, as the other upload routes already do. The web forwards them in E3-W08.
   
   **IT against an S3-compatible store** (MinIO with Testcontainers, inside `verify`):
   - a PUT with the returned headers succeeds;
   - the same PUT without `If-None-Match` fails with 403;
   - a second PUT to the same key fails, because of the condition.
2. **M17 · the client IP behind the proxy, and route matching (R-04-20, R-04-17).**
   - The limiter key and the consent `ipHash` come from `getRemoteAddr()`. Behind Caddy that is the proxy's address unless `TRUSTED_PROXY_PATTERN` is set, and its default is empty (`application.yml:49-53`).
     - In the `staging` and `prod` profiles, fail at startup when the pattern is empty. `local` and `test` keep working.
     - Fix `docs/DEPLOY.md:172-175`: it says `framework`, but the config is `native`. Document the variable next to the Caddy block.
   - `RateLimitFilter` (`:34`) matches the raw request URI. Match the decoded, normalised path, the one Spring routes, so that `POST /api/v1/signup/identity-%63hecks` and the `//` variants are limited like the plain route.
   
   Tests:
   - with `X-Forwarded-For` from a trusted proxy, the key is the client IP; from an untrusted peer, it is the peer;
   - the percent-encoded route is rate-limited. The audit expects it to get through the limit today: say whether it did before the fix;
   - a startup test for the fail-fast.
3. **M18 · a readmission does not overwrite the LEFT record (ruling E38, amends R-04-06).** Today an anonymous `POST /signup` with the DNI of a `LEFT` member overwrites that record in place (`SignupService.java:135-151`), with no before-image and no audit.
   - At submission, the person fields of the `LEFT` record are **not** modified:
     - the submitted values (the R-04-06 list: contact, address, payment method, and the new consent entries) wait in the signup as a pending payload;
     - the member goes `LEFT → PENDING` with `signup.readmission = true`, as today.
   - `GET /members/{id}/signup` returns both, the current values and the submitted ones, so D2 can show old vs new (E3-W07).
   - Validation applies the submitted values. The change is audited with the masked diff (R-14-09).
   - Rejection leaves the record exactly as it was: back to `LEFT` with its **original** `leftReason` and `leftAt`. It never becomes `SIGNUP_REJECTED`, which would move it to the short retention class of R-14-16 (b).
   - A readmission submission is audited (`SIGNUP_SUBMITTED`, masked, `origin = PUBLIC`).
   
   Tests: extend T-04-12.
   - After a readmission submission, the LEFT record's fields are unchanged.
   - Validation applies the submitted values.
   - Rejection restores `LEFT` with the original reason, date and data.
   - The audit entries exist, with masked diffs.
4. **The security minors**, each with a test:
   1. `signup.rateLimit` is read per club. Today the limits are literals in `RateLimits.java:30`.
   2. Per-recipient caps on N-39 (the identity-check mail) and on the applicant's N-01, so the anonymous routes cannot flood one address. If `signup.rateLimit` has no key for this, propose one (name, default) in the report and use a constant until the catalog has it.
   3. Consents accepted under impersonation are recorded with the actor (`actorAccountId`, `origin = BACKOFFICE`), never as the member's own acceptance.
   4. The anonymous signup routes (`identity-checks`, `family-group-lookups`, `upload-urls`, `uploads`) answer `422 SIGNUP_CLOSED` when `signup.enabled = false` or the club is not `ACTIVE`, like `POST /signup`.
   5. The family and identity lookups use indexed queries, with no full scan of the members. Their inputs are bounded (name lengths, list sizes) → `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`.
   6. The `SignupEdited` diffs in `domain_events` are masked like the audit (R-14-09).
   7. `IdempotencyFilter` retries a transient transaction error (a write conflict). Two concurrent submissions with different keys give one 201 and one `422 SIGNUP_ALREADY_PENDING`, never a 500. Test it with two threads.
   8. `maskedEmail` follows the R-04-05 shape («m•••a@e•••.cat»), and N-39 goes to exactly the address that `maskedEmail` masks.
   9. `docs/DEPLOY.md`: the bucket policy needs `s3:DeleteObject` for the orphan cleanup; add the proxy section of step 2.
5. **A stale `GET /signup` right after a change (web E3-W05, question 1).**
   - The configuration cache of `GET /signup` (60 s, `SignupService.java:30-32, 210-212`) is evicted only by the outbox consumers of `SignupConfigurationEvents`. After `PUT /parameters/signup.enabled {false}` → 200, two `GET /signup` 0.5 s later still answered `enabled: true` (E3-W05 log `01f`). The real-core test «closed form» is red in 5 of 8 runs.
   - Evict synchronously after commit in the writes that change the configuration: parameters, plans, prices, and the club's own data. Keep the async path as a backstop.
   - `POST /signup` and the anonymous routes decide `SIGNUP_CLOSED` on the committed parameter, never on the cached configuration.
   - List the other caches that are evicted only by outbox consumers. For each one that a user flow reads right after its own write, do the same, or say why not. (The dashboard is M11, already done in E3-T08.)
   
   IT, without any sleep: `PUT signup.enabled=false` → `GET /signup` at once → `enabled: false`, and `POST /signup` → `422 SIGNUP_CLOSED`.

### Assumptions
1. **S3 store: LocalStack, not MinIO.**
   - `minio/minio:RELEASE.2025-04-22T22-12-26Z` → «pull access denied for minio/minio, repository does not exist» (log `01`).
   - `quay.io/minio/minio:…` → 401 (log `02`).
   - So neither this machine nor the CI runner can start MinIO. The IT uses `localstack/localstack:3.8.1` with `SERVICES=s3`, `S3_SKIP_SIGNATURE_VALIDATION=0` and its default test credentials, and path-style addressing through a test `@Primary` `S3AttachmentStorage` (the production class).
   - It proves the three behaviours of the task on a SigV4-validating store: 200 with the headers, 403 without `If-None-Match`, 412 on a second PUT.
2. **The "IdempotencyFilter retry"** cannot re-run the filter chain: MockMvc refuses («This FilterChain has already been called!»), and in Tomcat it would skip the filters after it. So:
   - the two submission routes join the filter's existing `IdempotentOperation` branch (as bookings and activities do);
   - the controller runs the submission in `SignupTransactions`, which retries the whole transaction on a write conflict;
   - the idempotency row commits in the same transaction (sealed for anonymous callers).
   
   The result is the one the task asks for: one 201 and one `422 SIGNUP_ALREADY_PENDING`, never a 500. Exhausted attempts → `409 STALE_VERSION`, as in S07–S09.
3. **E38, what waits:** besides the R-04-06 list (contacts, address, payment method, new consent entries), the submitted name, surnames, gender and birth date also wait, and validation applies them.
   - They were overwritten before too. Dropping them would lose what the applicant typed.
   - D2 shows every changed field in `changedFields`.
   - The record keeps its `idDocument` (it matched).
   - The pending values live in `Member.readmissionRequest.submitted`, not inside `signup`, because `signup` is copied to the dogs, diffed by D2 edits and read by D1. A full IBAN never reaches those places. `readmissionRequest.previous` holds what a rejection restores: status, `leftAt`, `leftReason`, `leaveDate`, the claim and the original `signup` block.
4. **D2 edits of a pending readmission** (`PATCH /members/{id}`: person fields and payment method) edit the submitted values, not the LEFT record. Otherwise validation would silently overwrite the admin's correction.
5. **The mails of a pending readmission** (the applicant's N-01, a rejection's N-03) go to the record's primary address, which does not change until validation (question 1).
6. **Recipient cap:**
   - 3 per hour, per club, per notification and per recipient;
   - N-39 is keyed by the account (the link goes to its access address), the applicant's N-01 by the address;
   - the signup's own N-01 counts, and add-dog applicant copies too.
   
   `signup.rateLimit` has no key for it: a constant in `RateLimits`, with the catalog proposal below.
7. **Consents:** every new entry records `origin`: `PUBLIC` anonymous; `APP` for the member, with their `actorAccountId`; `BACKOFFICE` under impersonation, with the admin's `actorAccountId`. An impersonated acceptance still counts in the ledger (e.g. `consentsUpToDate`). The rule asks that the actor be recorded.
8. **Audit origin:** `AuditOrigin.PUBLIC` is new. Only the anonymous readmission submission writes it; every other entry keeps its origin.
9. **Step 5, the other caches evicted only by outbox consumers** (M11's dashboard excluded):
   - the club configuration and host caches (`PlatformConfiguration`): marked, so a domain or status change resolves at once. The configuration was already evicted synchronously by the parameter and club writers;
   - the template (`TemplateQuery`, 60 s): marked; its own writes already evicted after commit;
   - the training grid (`TrainingGridCache`): marked;
   - the public activities (`PublicActivityService`, 300 s): marked;
   - `BookableClassesCache` is not evicted by consumers at all (a TTL, plus `WeekOpeningJob`), so it is not in scope;
   - the rate-limit buckets are not data.
   
   The mechanism: `DomainEventHandler.evictsAfterCommit()`. `MongoEventPublisher` delivers the marked handlers on this instance right after the commit, and the outbox delivery stays the backstop. The dashboard handlers are **not** marked, because they register their own after-commit synchronization, which would never fire inside `afterCommit`.
