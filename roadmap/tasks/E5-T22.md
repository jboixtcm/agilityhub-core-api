## Steps
1. **`priceLabel` on `GET /signup` (E4-W12 question 1, S05 R-05-19).** Each plan carries `priceLabel`: `Plan.texts.priceLabel` in the reader's locale, when the plan has one. The Cànic's Teràpia reads «condicions i cost segons cada cas». IT.
2. **The member's own plan in add-dog mode (E4-W12 question 2, R-04-09, M8).**
   - `GET /signup` for a member includes the member's own plan in `plans`, marked `current: true`, even when the public offer hides it (the Cànic's family plan). The other plans keep `current: false` or absent.
   - A member without a plan (instructors migrated without one, B34) gets the offered plans, none `current`. `POST /me/dogs/signup` then requires `planIdRequested`: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` on `planIdRequested` without it.
   - ITs for both, on the Cànic seed's family member.
3. **`waitlistRank` (E5-T20 reviews #1 and #5).**
   - D7's list sets it only on a row whose `state` is `WAITLISTED`: `null` otherwise, even if a promotion happens between the page read and the rank read.
   - `GET /me/activities` computes the ranks once per activity, not once per waiting row.
   - IT and a unit test.
4. **`fields` on every universal list (E5-T20 review #3 and question 2, `CONVENCIONS_API.md` §4).**
   - Every list that publishes `x-fields` honours `fields`: it leaves out the unrequested keys, always sends the row id, and its item schema requires only the row id. This covers `/weeks`, `/class-sessions`, `/ring-blocks`, `/training-bookings`, `/bookings` and `/jobs/{name}/runs`.
   - A list that cannot honour `fields` stops accepting the parameter and publishes no `x-fields`. This applies to the three contract-only platform lists (`/platform/audit-entries`, `/platform/erasure-requests`, `/platform/security-events`) until they are implemented.
   - Extend `ListFieldsContractIT` to check, on every universal list, that a request with `fields` gets sparse items that conform to the snapshot.
5. **`id` on D7's lists (E5-T20 review #4).** `id` goes into the `x-filterable` of `GET /activities` and `GET /activities/{id}/registrations`, which already accept it. Contract test.
6. **Docs (E5-T20 reviews #2 and #6).**
   - The `ring-conflicts` description, the E5-T20 report and its changelog entry stop saying «like the publication». The preview answers `400 INVALID_TIME_RANGE` when there is no window to block, while the publication refuses those cases earlier, with `422 ACTIVITY_INCOMPLETE`.
   - The `ActivityIT` comment near `:536` names `assertNoEnd` as the proof.

  - `GET /me/activities` reads the waiting entries of all its activities in one query: `ActivityProjection.waitlistRanks(Collection)` and `ActivityRegistrationRepository.waiting(Collection)`. The ranks are computed once per activity.

  - `GET /class-sessions/{id}/bookings` keeps a whole item: `ClassBookingItem`, the former `BookingListItem`.
  - `@ListContract(acceptsFields = false)` removes `fields` from the three contract-only platform lists.
  - The four fixes above.
