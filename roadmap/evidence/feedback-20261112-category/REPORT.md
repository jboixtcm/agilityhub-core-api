## Implementation

- The existing tenant-scoped `/dogs` list supports `levelId:eq:<id>` and `status:eq:ACTIVE`, sorted by `levelAssignedAt,asc` and `name,asc`. Reuse the existing paginated endpoint; no hard cap or new endpoint is introduced.

- Dog birth dates are readable by ADMIN and INSTRUCTOR, matching the existing dog-detail permission. Member birth dates remain inaccessible to instructors. No member data, permissions or payment settings change.
