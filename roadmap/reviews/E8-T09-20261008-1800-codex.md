Even another `compensate()` call returns immediately because `refund:<paymentId>` already exists. This leaves point 4 and R-12-20’s automatic cancellation refund underpaid. Track the outstanding compensation and reconsider it when a contributing refund fails, with idempotent additional commands. Extend the partial-refund regression to cover this ordering.
