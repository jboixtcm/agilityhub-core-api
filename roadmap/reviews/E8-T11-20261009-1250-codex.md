# Review E8-T11 — needs changes

## Findings

1. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/PaymentRefunds.java:281` — **Pending dashboard refunds still permit avoidable double compensation.** A `pending` refund is stored in `stripe_refunds`, but neither adjusts the credit nor contributes to `ledger().reserved()`, which reads only local operations. Example: €12 captured → €12 CREDIT → dashboard refund of €5 reported pending → billing consumes the €12 credit → refund succeeds. The member receives €17; the intervention only reports the overpayment afterward. This leaves step 3 and E101’s pending-refund correction incomplete. Include known external pending refunds in reservation accounting, without double-counting linked operations, and adjust the unbilled credit before billing. Test pending → billing → success and failure. The generator’s independently calculated ledger repeats the same omission.

2. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/PaymentRefunds.java:193` — **Failed refunds for genuinely expired checkouts bypass the new admin notification.** An expired booking’s payment remains `CANCELLED`, without the late intent in `stripe.paymentIntentId`; `lateBooking()` creates a `REFUND_LATE` operation instead. After its failure, both webhook reconciliation and exhausted-refusal handling call `reconsiderCompensation(intent)`, whose `upfront.forIntent(intent)` lookup finds no payment. The refund stops without `PAYMENT_REFUND_INTERVENTION` or N-55, leaving the member’s money outstanding. This violates step 1’s requirement to surface necessary manual work. Resolve late operations through their payment/checkout target and record the outstanding amount using the late capture. Add an actual expired-checkout failure test: the generator’s “late confirmation” starts with an already `PAID`, intent-linked payment and misses this path.
