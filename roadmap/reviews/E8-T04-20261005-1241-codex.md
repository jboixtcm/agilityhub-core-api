# Review E8-T04 — needs changes

## Findings

1. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/StripeWebhooks.java:110` — Refund settlement depends exclusively on `charge.refunds.data`. Stripe no longer expands this field by default; a valid `charge.refunded` without it becomes permanently `IGNORED`, leaving `Collection.refunds`, `Invoice.refundedTotal` and upfront refunds unchanged. This breaks R-12-20/21 and task step 7. Fetch the relevant refunds through the provider before the transaction, preserving durable correlation, and test a webhook without embedded refunds. [Stripe’s API change](https://docs.stripe.com/changelog/2022-11-15/deprecates-charges-auto-expand).

2. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/PaymentRefunds.java:95` — A returned refund ID completes the operation regardless of its status. Meanwhile, `StripeWebhooks` skips pending refunds and ignores `refund.updated`/`refund.failed`. Stripe supports both pending→succeeded and succeeded→failed transitions, so refunds can remain unrecorded or remain recorded after failure. R-12-20 requires the ledger to reflect the refund outcome. Persist and reconcile the refund lifecycle, including failed reservations, and add both asynchronous transition tests. [Stripe’s documented refund scenarios](https://docs.stripe.com/testing#refunds).

3. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/PaymentBookingCancellations.java:34` — Cancellation compensation is independent of late-payment compensation. If Checkout marks the upfront payment `PAID`, then P7 cancels the still-`PAYMENT_PENDING` booking before `UpfrontPaymentSucceeded` is consumed, that consumer schedules `late:<paymentIntent>`. The cancellation consumer also schedules `booking-cancel:<paymentId>`, or creates a credit under `CREDIT`. This produces two refund requests or a refund **plus** a credit, violating step 12’s once-per-provider-payment requirement and R-12-20. Distinguish cancellation before booking confirmation from cancellation of a confirmed booking, and coordinate compensation by captured payment. Test both consumer orders with `REFUND` and `CREDIT`.

4. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/PaymentRetryPolicy.java:30` — Exhausting a charge operation only terminates its recovery record. `CardPayments.queue()` has already committed the collection as `SUBMITTED` and invoice as `COLLECTING`; a provider rejection before creating a PaymentIntent therefore leaves both stuck, with no future webhook. `/retry` rejects the invoice because it is not `FAILED`, and another run-charge command selects the exhausted operation but silently does nothing. This leaves steps 5–6/R-12-13/18 incomplete and omits `InvoiceFailed` notifications. Resolve definitive submission failures into the business failure state; provide reconciliation for uncertain outcomes. Test exhaustion followed by corrected-provider recovery.

5. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/CheckoutService.java:255` — The late-signup branch explicitly accepts rows still `CHECKOUT_PENDING` for this session, then calls `UpfrontPayments.pending()`, which rejects every status except `DUE`/`PARTIAL` (`UpfrontPayments.java:199`). A paid completion after the local deadline but before the expiry worker releases the rows consequently fails and can exhaust inbox recovery without settlement or refund. This contradicts step 13’s explicit `CHECKOUT_PENDING → PAID` requirement. Reuse rows already pending for this session, reclaim only payable rows, and test completion after the deadline without first calling `expire()`.
