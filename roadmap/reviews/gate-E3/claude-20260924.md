# Gate E3 audit — fail

**Scope:** stage E3 = S04 (public signup, add-dog, D2 validation) and the dashboard part of S14 (D1, `/dashboard`, `/dashboard/counters`), in both repos, at the frozen commits api `20552c6` and web `5d371d3`.

**Method:**
- Six independent read-only sub-audits: api rules R-04-xx; api contract, events and tests; security and privacy; D1 api; the public signup screens; D2 and D1 web.
- **The organizer confirmed every blocker and major below in the code.** Some were also confirmed in the gate's own screenshots.
- Minors are listed as the sub-audits reported them, with their file:line. They are verified by reading, not by execution.
- Nothing was executed; the executor audit runs E3-T07 (api) and E3-W05 (web) provide the fresh execution evidence.

**Verdict: the E3 gate does not pass.** The happy path works against the real core (the E1–E3 e2e is green), but:
- NIE and passport applicants cannot submit at all;
- the payment step shows broken texts and misses the total;
- the family plan cannot be proposed or used;
- several D2 back-office actions fail or lose data.

The organizer ticked gate E3 (front) on 24-09 after checking D2 only. The 18/19 screenshots of the same run already showed the raw placeholders and the missing total. **The tick is withdrawn until the fixes land.**

## Findings

### Blocker

**B1 · blocker** `web/apps/clubs/src/SignupPage.tsx:1359` (the `person` spread; the correct builder is `identityDocument()` at `:376-389`)
- **Defect:** `POST /signup` sends `draft.person.idDocument` as-is. Its `type` stays at the initial `"DNI"`, and the passport field is never sent. Only the identity check uses `identityDocument()`.
- **Spec:** S04 §2 row 16, R-04-01, §6 body.
- **Failure:**
  - A NIE applicant (`X1234567L`) passes step 16: the check sends `NIE`.
  - At step 19 the body carries `{type:"DNI", value:"X1234567L"}`. The api then runs `SpanishCountryProfile.validateIdDocument("DNI", …)` (`api/…/platform/domain/SpanishCountryProfile.java:20-28`), which fails with `400 INVALID_ID_DOCUMENT`. The screen shows the generic error.
  - A passport-only applicant sends `{type:"DNI", value:""}` and gets `400`.
  - Neither can ever finish. Every test, the e2e and the MSW handler use DNIs only.
- **Fix:** build the body's document with `identityDocument()`. Add Vitest bodies for NIE and passport, and one real-core NIE e2e.

### Major — public signup (web)

**M1 · major** `web/apps/clubs/src/SignupPage.tsx:1331,1343,1369,1382`
- **Defect:** every submit sends a fresh `Idempotency-Key` (`crypto.randomUUID()` inline). The draft is deleted before the checkout call, and `signupToken` lives only in a closure.
- **Spec:** CONVENCIONS_API §7 (retries from the phone), R-04-26, R-04-27.
- **Failure:**
  - The 201 is lost on a flaky connection, or the checkout call fails. The user taps again with a new key and gets `422 SIGNUP_ALREADY_PENDING`, with no success page and no payment.
  - In add-dog mode the retry answers `422 DOG_CHIP_ALREADY_REGISTERED` for the member's own dog.
- **Fix:** one key per payload, kept in the draft and reset when the draft changes. Keep `memberId` and the token until the flow ends.

**M2 · major** `web/apps/clubs/src/SignupPage.tsx:451-463` (step 16) and `:1389-1404` (step 19)
- **Defect:** step 16 maps only `VALIDATION_ERROR.details.fieldErrors`. Step 19 handles five codes, and every other error, 400 field errors included, becomes «No s'ha pogut completar l'acció.», with no field and no way back to the step that owns it.
- **Spec:** §2 («400 del back mapejat a camps»), CATALEG_ERRORS (fronts map by code).
- **Failure:** the api answers top-level codes with empty `details`: `INVALID_ID_DOCUMENT`, `INVALID_IBAN`, `INVALID_PHONE`, `PLAN_NOT_AVAILABLE`, `DOG_DOCUMENT_REQUIRED`, `FAMILY_HOLDER_NOT_FOUND`, `FILE_NOT_FOUND`, `MEMBER_ALREADY_EXISTS`.
  - An IBAN typo leaves the applicant stuck at step 19 with a generic error.
  - Field paths such as `emails[0]` are not normalised to the form's keys.
- **Fix:** fall back to `t('errors:'+code)`; map codes and paths to fields and steps, and navigate there; add the local ES checks (postal code, birth date, `birthMonth`, IBAN mod-97); align the MSW error shapes with the api.

**M3 · major** `web/apps/clubs/src/SignupPage.tsx:1517-1573`
- **Defect:** «Total a pagar al club» is missing.
- **Spec:** §2 row 19, §10 key `signup:step4.total`, mockup 19, T-04-32.
- **Evidence:** `E3-W03/19-payment-core-375.png` shows «Entrada 100 €» and the two options, but no total.
- **Fix:** a display-only total (entry fee + the selected option, or the pack price) that is never sent. Assert it in T-04-32.

**M4 · major** `web/packages/i18n/src/locales/*/signup.json:111-112`, used at `SignupPage.tsx:1562-1564`
- **Defect:** the option labels are fixed: TODAY = «(mig mes)», ALTERNATIVE = «(mes complet)».
- **Spec:** R-04-15: for d < 16 it is A «Alta avui (mes complet)» and B «Alta el dia 16 (mig mes)». §10 names four keys (`todayFull|todayHalf|splitDayHalf|nextMonthFull`).
- **Failure:** on days 1–15 of every month, both labels contradict the amounts next to them.
- **Fix:** pick the key from `upfront.today` and `firstMonthSplitDay`, add the add-dog keys, and test 05-08, 16-08 and 31-12.

**M5 · major** `web/apps/clubs/src/SignupPage.tsx:1517-1560`
- **Defect:** the «Pagament inicial» card ignores the selected plan type.
- **Spec:** R-04-14: `PACK` → the PACK line + `ENTRY_FEE` only if > 0; `SINGLE_CLASS` → `ENTRY_FEE` only if > 0.
- **Failure:**
  - It always shows the monthly first-month options, computed from the first monthly plan.
  - It never shows the PACK price.
  - It prints «Entrada 0,00 €» for plans without an entry fee.
  - With «Pack 6» selected, the applicant sees monthly options they will never be charged, and not the 135 € they will.
- **Fix:** hide zero lines, show the options only for `MONTHLY` plans with a price, and add the PACK line.

### Major — signup configuration, plans, payments and notifications (api)

**M6 · major** `api/…/clubs/census/application/SignupService.java:221-237` (`textParameter`)
- **Defect:** `GET /signup` returns `texts.paymentDay` and `texts.familyGroupIntro` with their catalog placeholders still in them (`{deadlineDay}` at `catalog.yaml:123`, `{twoDogsMonthlyFee}` at `:1176`). The Cànic seed does not override them.
- **Spec:** §6 `texts{…}`, §9, §10 (the back returns resolved values).
- **Evidence:** `E3-W03/19-payment-core-375.png` shows «abans del dia {deadlineDay}», and `18-family-core-375.png` shows «seria de {twoDogsMonthlyFee}». Every applicant sees this, in all three languages.

**M7 · major** `api/…/SignupService.java:226`
- **Defect:** `paymentMethods[MANUAL].instructions` returns `signup.text.cashConditions` (the semester cash rules). It should return `CLUB.paymentProviders.MANUAL.instructions`, which says how to pay the upfront.
- **Spec:** §2 row 19, §10, model §2.
- **Evidence:** screenshot 19 shows «Pagament en efectiu: la quota es paga per semestres…» under «Pagament inicial», so the applicant is never told how to pay the entry fee.

**M8 · major** `api/…/clubs/signup/domain/SignupPlanCatalog.java:18`, `api/…/clubs/signup/application/SignupPolicy.java:43-47`, `SignupService.java:198,229,263-276`
- **Defect:** the only "plan available" check is the public signup list (active ∧ `showOnSignup` ∧ module), and it is used for proposals, validation, D2 and add-dog too. The Cànic's `ABONAT_FAMILIAR` has `showOnSignup: false` (`seeds/club-canic.yaml:382`).
- **Spec:** R-04-13 (the family fare is proposed and the admin decides), R-04-25.
- **Failure:**
  - The family fare is never proposed: `proposedPlan` searches the visible plans only. Choosing it at validation returns `422 PLAN_NOT_AVAILABLE`.
  - Add-dog fails for every member on the family plan: `addDog` → `policy.require(member.planId)`. On days 1–25, `GET /signup` for such a member fails as well.
  - If a requested plan is hidden while a signup is pending, D2 no longer loads.
  - The gate's D2 screenshot validated a FOUND family claim at «Abonat · 60 €/mes».
- **Fix:** separate "offered at signup" from "assignable" (active ∧ module) in `proposedPlan`, `addDog`, `additionalFee` and `review`.

**M9 · major** `api/…/clubs/census/application/SignupNotifications.java:34` with `messages_*.properties:343-344`
- **Defect:** the admins' N-01 (EMAIL + APP) reuses the applicant template, «Hem rebut la teva sol·licitud per a {dogs}. {club_name} la revisarà.»
- **Spec:** §8, CATALEG_NOTIFICACIONS N-01 (ADMINS → APP+EMAIL, variables `member_name`, `dogs`, `plan_name`).
- **Failure:** every admin is told "we received **your** request", without the applicant's name or plan.
- **Fix:** add admin keys that use `member_name`/`plan_name` with a D2 link, and assert both bodies.

**M10 · major** `api/…/payments/application/UpfrontPayments.java:22-26,63-79`
- **Defect:** payment rows are selected by `memberId` + `dogId` only, so a readmission that reactivates the same dog (R-04-07) inherits the rows of the earlier application. Separately, `replace()` (a plan change at validation):
  - overwrites `amountDue` of a partly paid row with `amountPaid` and marks it PAID;
  - cancels `CHECKOUT_PENDING` rows while a provider session is open.
- **Spec:** §5 («Els imports no es modifiquen mai sobre un registre existent»; a plan change cancels only DUE lines), R-04-06/07.
- **Failure:**
  - A member who left and is readmitted with the same dog shows the old entry fee as paid on D2, and the due amount is computed against it.
  - With Stripe (E8), a payment completed after a plan change matches no row and is never recorded.
- **Fix:** scope the rows to their submission, cancel only DUE rows, and never rewrite amounts.

**M11 · major** `api/…/clubs/dashboard/application/DashboardEvents.java:16-35` with `OutboxDispatcher.java:38` (1 s poll), `web/apps/clubs-admin/src/dashboard/SignupReviewPage.tsx:304-310,369`
- **Defect:** the D1 and menu-counter caches are evicted only by async outbox consumers. D2 warms the cache (it calls `GET /dashboard` for `warnDays`) and then navigates to `/tauler` immediately after a validation.
- **Spec:** R-14-01, T-14-11, S04 §2 D2 («retorn a D1»).
- **Failure:**
  - D1 shows the signup just validated as still pending, with the old counts.
  - Clicking its [VALIDA] opens D2, which answers `409 INVALID_STATE` (`SignupService.review`) and shows the generic load error.
  - The real-core e2e hides this with `waitForTimeout(1_000)` and a detour through `/abonats` (`e2e/core/e3-signup.spec.ts:400-401`).
- **Fix:** invalidate synchronously after commit in the commands (validate, reject, submit, add-dog); D2 reads `warnDays` without touching `/dashboard`; D2 maps a non-pending member to a clear message.

### Major — D2 back office (web)

**M12 · major** `web/apps/clubs-admin/src/dashboard/SignupReviewPage.tsx:150-158`
- **Defect:** the D2 dog `PATCH` sends `signup.version`, which is the member's version. `DogService` compares the dog's own version (`api/…/DogService.java:34`), and `SignupDogView` publishes none.

**M14 · major** `SignupReviewPage.tsx:185-212`

**M15 · major** `SignupReviewPage.tsx:418-426,471,334-345`

### Major — security and privacy (api; for the release)

**M16 · major** `api/…/clubs/census/api/SignupController.java:146` (`UploadUrl` without `headers`), `web/apps/clubs/src/SignupPage.tsx:879-883`
- **Defect:** the S3 presigned PUT is signed with `If-None-Match: *` and the content type (`S3AttachmentStorage.java:13-17`). The signup response drops the headers the service returns (`AttachmentService.java:58`), so the browser sends only `Content-Type`.
- **Spec:** R-04-08, T-04-13/30.
- **Failure:** with S3 (staging and production), every vaccination-card upload in signup and add-dog fails with 403. Local storage ignores the header, so every test is green. The other uploaders forward the headers correctly.
- **Fix:** return `headers` and forward them; add an IT against an S3-compatible store (MinIO).

**M17 · major** `api/src/main/resources/application.yml:49-53` and `api/…/shared/api/RateLimitFilter.java:34`
- **Defect:** two problems:
  - The limiter key and the consent `ipHash` use `getRemoteAddr()`. Behind Caddy that is the proxy's IP unless `TRUSTED_PROXY_PATTERN` is set; its default is empty, with no fail-fast. `docs/DEPLOY.md:172-175` contradicts the config (`framework` vs `native`).
  - Routes are matched on the raw, undecoded URI, while Spring matches decoded paths.
- **Spec:** R-04-20, R-04-17, CONVENCIONS_API §9.
- **Failure:**
  - Deployed with defaults, all applicants of a club share one bucket: 5 bot posts per hour lock the form for everyone. Every consent carries the same `ipHash`.
  - `POST /api/v1/signup/identity-%63hecks` is expected to bypass the limit. This is inferred from the matching code; a test must confirm it.
- **Fix:** fail at startup in staging/prod without the pattern; fix DEPLOY.md; match on the decoded, normalised path; add regression tests.

**M18 · major** `api/…/SignupService.java:135-151`
- **Defect:** an anonymous readmission (DNI only) overwrites the LEFT member's record in place at submission: name, birth date, gender, contacts, address and payment. There is no before-image and no audit, since `submit` is not audited.
- **Spec:** R-04-06 lists only contact, address, payment and consents; R-14-09; GDPR art. 5(1)(d)(f).
- **Failure:**
  - Someone who knows an ex-member's DNI rewrites that record.
  - If the signup is rejected, the original data is lost, and `leftReason` becomes `SIGNUP_REJECTED`, which moves the record into the short retention class.
- **Fix:** keep the LEFT record untouched until validation (store the readmission as a pending payload), show old vs new on D2, and audit with masking.
