# Review E8-T09 — needs changes

## Findings

1. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/PaymentRefunds.java:95` — CREDIT compensation still loses money when an earlier partial refund fails. A €12 capture with a pending €2 refund produces only €10 credit on cancellation. If the €2 refund subsequently fails, `reconsiderCompensation()` finds no refund-compensation marker, and another cancellation call returns immediately because the credit already exists. This leaves €2 uncompensated, contrary to R-12-20 and task point 4. Track the outstanding CREDIT obligation and reconcile the difference safely, including when the original credit has already been invoiced. Add failure/cancellation regressions for CREDIT.

2. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/PaymentRefunds.java:198` — Failed cancellation refunds generate an unlimited chain of replacement commands. Every distinct `failed`/`canceled` refund triggers `compensateRemaining()`, which creates another key (`refund:<payment>:1`, `:2`, etc.) with a fresh retry budget. The exclusion at lines 172–174 applies only to request exceptions, not webhook failures. This bypasses the bounded recovery requirement in E8-T04’s round-2 ruling, point 5, and can repeatedly replace an explicitly canceled refund. Bound recovery across the compensation obligation and retain a terminal outcome requiring intervention. Test repeated failures of the compensation itself; Stripe also documents alternative reimbursement for [failed refunds](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds#failed-refunds).

3. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/payments/application/PaymentRefunds.java:111` — Supplement creation can prevent authoritative refund reconciliation. For an already accepted, deferred refund-failure webhook, disabling Stripe before recovery makes this call enter `upfront()` and fail its `provider.require(REFUND)` check. The entire reconciliation transaction rolls back, including reversal and reservation release. After inbox retries exhaust, `PaymentRetryState.failed()` deletes `work`, so re-enabling Stripe cannot recover that event. This violates R-12-20/21 and the reservation-recovery objective. Commit reconciliation independently of provider availability; persist the outstanding compensation for later execution. Add an accepted-event → disable-provider → recovery regression.
