## Findings

1. **major** `src/main/java/com/agilityhub/core/clubs/training/application/TrainingSlotLocks.java:35` — S05’s locking horizon becomes incomplete when its transaction crosses club-local midnight. With `bookingWindowDays=3`, a ring update starting on October 5 locks slots through October 8. A concurrent booking after midnight can book October 9 while still reading the ring’s previous state. That booking touches no slot document locked by S05, so both transactions can commit, leaving an ACTIVE booking on a disabled ring. This violates R-09-04/R-09-13 and Round 2 point 3. Make the locking protocol cover every slot that can become bookable during the transaction’s lifetime, and add a lanes-off test that holds the actual catalog update open while advancing the clock across midnight.
